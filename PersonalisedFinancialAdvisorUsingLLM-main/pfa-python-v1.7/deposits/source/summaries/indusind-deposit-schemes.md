## Recurring Deposit (RD) Key Features & Rules

IndusInd Bank offers standard RDs alongside unique value-added savings programs to accommodate systematic savers.

* **Minimum Monthly Installment:** High accessibility with a low entry limit of **₹500 per month**.
* **Flexible Tenures:** Open accounts for tenures spanning **6 months to 120 months** (10 years) in standard multiples.
* **High Yield Ranges:** Standard recurring deposits yield from **4.75% to 7.50% p.a.** for general depositors, and up to **8.25% p.a.** for senior citizens.
* **Product Variations:**
  * **Standard Recurring Deposit:** Classic systematic savings capturing fixed card rates.
  * **Value Added Recurring Deposit:** Special partnerships bundling accumulated principal with downstream benefits (such as travel, jewelry, or shopping vouchers on maturity).
* **Premature Closure:** Early termination of the RD is permitted, subject to nominal penal deductions based on the period elapsed, with interest paid at the applicable rate for the exact tenure held.
