## Recurring Deposit (RD) Key Features & Rules

PNB recurring deposits combine the flexibility of systematic monthly savings with the high yields typically reserved for lump-sum term deposits.

* **Minimum Deposit:** Open an account starting from **₹100 per month**, and thereafter in multiples of ₹1.
* **Maximum Limit:** Up to **₹25 Lakhs** per customer per month.
* **Flexible Tenures:** Ranges from **6 months to 120 months** (10 years), allowing precise alignments with your goals.
* **Delayed Installment Policy:** Delayed installments can be deposited before maturity by paying a nominal penalty of **₹1 per ₹100** per month.
* **Maturity Grace:** The RD payment is due on the exact maturity date or one month after the deposit of the last installment, whichever is later.
* **Premature Closure:** Premature withdrawal is permitted but attracts a **1.00% penal interest** deduction from the applicable card rate.
* **Loan Against Deposit:** Depositors can avail of loans or overdrafts up to **90% of the accumulated RD balance**.
* **Senior Citizen Incentives:** Seniors (60-79 years) get an extra **50 bps** premium, while Super Seniors (80+) enjoy an extra **80 bps** premium on deposits under ₹3 Crore.
