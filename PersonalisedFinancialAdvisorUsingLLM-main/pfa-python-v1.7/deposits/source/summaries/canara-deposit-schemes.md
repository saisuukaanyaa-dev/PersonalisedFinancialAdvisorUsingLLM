# Canara Bank Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

Canara Bank, a leading public sector financial institution in India, provides robust deposit schemes featuring quarterly compounding, specialized senior citizen benefits, and high-yield medium-term tenures.

## Fixed Deposits & Recurring Deposit Interest Rates
*(For Retail Deposits below ₹3 Crore, w.e.f. late 2025 / September 2026)*

Canara Bank maintains uniform interest pricing across its standard Fixed Deposits and Recurring Deposits for corresponding tenures:

| Tenure Bucket | General Citizens (% p.a.) | Senior Citizens (% p.a.) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **1 Year Only** | **7.00%** | **7.50%** |
| **Above 1 Year to less than 2 Years** | **7.00%** | **7.50%** |
| **2 Years & above to less than 3 Years** | 6.70% | 7.20% |
| **3 Years & above to less than 5 Years** | 6.50% | 7.00% |
| **5 Years & above to 10 Years** | 6.50% | 7.00% |

*Note: Special high-yield term products like the 444-day Canara Green Deposit scheme and specialized non-callable structures are launched periodically at discretionary rates.*

## Recurring Deposit (RD) Key Features & Rules

Canara Bank's RD scheme is an ideal systematic investment tool for households looking to accumulate capital with guaranteed, risk-free returns.

* **Minimum Monthly Installment:** Start an account with as little as **₹50 per month** in any branch.
* **No Upper Limit:** There is no maximum cap on the monthly installment amount.
* **Compounding Frequency:** Interest is compounded **every quarter** (using the industry-standard formula where the first quarter is calculated as simple interest and subsequent quarters accumulate compound yield).
* **Premature Closure Penalty:** A **1.00% penalty** is levied for premature closure of RDs. Prematurely closed accounts earn interest at **1.00% below the rate** prevailing on the deposit date for the period run, or 1.00% below the contracted rate, whichever is lower. No interest is paid on RDs closed before completion of the 7th day.
* **Delayed Installments:** Delayed monthly installments can be updated as per standard bank penalty tables prior to final maturity.
* **Loan and Overdraft Support:** Depositors can avail of cash credit or loans up to **90% of the accumulated RD balance** to meet urgent liquidity requirements without breaking the deposit.
