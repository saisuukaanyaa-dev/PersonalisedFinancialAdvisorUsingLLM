# ICICI Bank Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

ICICI Bank is recognized for incorporating high technological flexibility into its deposit products, highlighted by goal-based flexible recurring plans and specialized senior citizen programs.

## 1. Fixed Deposits (FDs)
Standard interest rates for Retail Fixed Deposits (less than ₹5 Crore):
*   **Standard Rates:** Range from **2.75% to 6.50% p.a.** for standard depositors, and **3.25% to 7.10% p.a.** for senior citizens.
*   **ICICI Bank Golden Years FD:** A special term deposit scheme curated for senior citizens for tenures of **5 to 10 years**, offering a preferential rate of **6.55% p.a.** (which is up to 80 bps higher than standard tenures).

## 2. Recurring Deposits (RDs)
ICICI Bank recurring deposit tenures range from 6 months to 10 years (in multiples of 3 months).

### Standard RD Interest Rates (w.e.f. December 29, 2025)
| Maturity Period | General Public (p.a.) | Senior Citizens (p.a.) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Up to 6 months | 4.50% | 5.00% |
| Above 6 months to 9 months | 5.50% | 6.00% |
| Above 9 months to 15 months | 6.25% | 6.75% |
| Above 15 months to 2 years | 6.30% | 6.80% |
| Above 2 years to 3 years | 6.45% | 6.95% |
| Above 3 years to 5 years | **6.50%** | **7.10%** |
| Above 5 years to 10 years | 6.50% | 7.00% |

*Note: The highest standard RD yield is **6.50% p.a.** achieved at the 36-month tenure.*

### Flexible & Specialty Deposit Innovations
*   **iWish Deposits:** A highly flexible goal-based recurring savings program. Unlike traditional RDs that levy strict penalties for delayed installments, iWish allows customers to save variable amounts periodically, yielding up to **7.10% p.a.**
*   **Delightful Deposits:** Specialty RDs established in partnership with prominent brands (such as Tanishq, Croma, MakeMyTrip, and Thomas Cook) designed for targeted milestone savings.

### Important RD Account Rules
*   **Minimum Deposit:** **₹500 monthly** (with increases in multiples of ₹100).
*   **Late Installment Penalty:** ₹12 per ₹1,000 monthly overdue.
*   **Premature Closure Rules:** No interest is paid on RDs closed within 7 days of opening (without penalty). Beyond that, premature closure interest is calculated at the applicable rate for the period held, subject to a penalty of **0.50%** (under 1 year) or **1.00%** (1 year and above).
