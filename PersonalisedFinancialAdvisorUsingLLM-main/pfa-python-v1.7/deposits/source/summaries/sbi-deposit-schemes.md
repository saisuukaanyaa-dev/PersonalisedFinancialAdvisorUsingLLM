# State Bank of India (SBI) Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

State Bank of India (SBI), the largest public sector bank in India, offers stable deposit rates alongside unique promotional schemes that reward longer-term savings and specific demographics.

## 1. Fixed Deposits (FDs)
Standard interest rates for Retail Domestic Term Deposits (below ₹3 Crore) w.e.f. December 15, 2025:

| Tenors | General Public (p.a.) | Senior Citizens (p.a.) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| 7 days to 45 days | 3.05% | 3.55% |
| 46 days to 179 days | 4.90% | 5.40% |
| 180 days to 210 days | 5.65% | 6.15% |
| 211 days to < 1 year | 5.90% | 6.40% |
| 1 Year to < 2 years | 6.25% | 6.75% |
| 2 years to < 3 years | 6.40% | 6.90% |
| 3 years to < 5 years | 6.30% | 6.80% |
| 5 years to 10 years | 6.05% | 7.05%* |

*\*Includes the additional 50 bps premium under the "SBI We-care" deposit scheme.*

### Special/Promotional FD Schemes
*   **Amrit Vrishti (444 Days):** A highly popular specific-tenor scheme open for subscription until March 31, 2025.
    *   *Peak Historical Yield:* General Public: **7.25% p.a.** | Senior Citizens: **7.75% p.a.** | Super Senior Citizens (80+): **7.85% p.a.**
    *   *Latest Card Revision (w.e.f Dec 15, 2025):* The baseline rate has been adjusted to **6.45% p.a.** for the general public, with senior/super-senior citizens remaining eligible for additional interest premiums.
*   **SBI Patrons:** Designed exclusively for Super Senior Citizens (80 years and above). Provides an additional **10 bps premium** over the applicable senior citizen rate across all tenures. *(Not applicable to RDs, Green FDs, Tax Savings, MODs, Capgain, or Non-Callable deposits).*
*   **SBI We-care:** A special term deposit for Senior Citizens offering a 50 bps premium for tenures between 5 and 10 years (yielding **7.05% p.a.** under current revisions).
*   **Sarvottam (Non-Callable):** Tailored for bulk domestic retail term deposits from ₹1 Crore to < ₹3 Crore. Offers higher yield margins with a strict lock-in (no premature withdrawals allowed):
    *   *1 Year:* **6.55% p.a.** (General) | **7.05% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   *2 Years:* **6.80% p.a.** (General) | **7.30% p.a.** (Senior)
*   **SBI Green Rupee Term Deposit (SGRTD):** Designed to support eco-friendly projects. Available for specific tenors of **1111, 1777, and 2222 days** priced at standard card rates.

## 2. Recurring Deposits (RDs)
SBI provides disciplined monthly savings tenures from 12 months to 120 months.
*   **1 Year to 2 Years:** **6.80% p.a.** (General) | **7.30% p.a.** (Senior)
*   **2 Years to 3 Years:** **7.00% p.a.** (General) | **7.50% p.a.** (Senior)
*   **3 Years to 5 Years:** **6.50% to 6.75% p.a.**
*   **Minimum Entry Deposit:** Low threshold of **₹100 monthly**.
