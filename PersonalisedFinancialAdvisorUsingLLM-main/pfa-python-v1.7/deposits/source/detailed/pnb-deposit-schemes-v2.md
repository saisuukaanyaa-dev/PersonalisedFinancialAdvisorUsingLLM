## 2. Comprehensive Breakdown of Individual Schemes

### 📌 A. PNB Sugam Term Deposit Scheme
A highly flexible, high-liquidity fixed deposit product that eliminates the standard premature penalty on partial withdrawals.
*   **Core Feature:** The depositor can withdraw money from this account in multiples of **₹1** (subject to a minimum of ₹1,000 at a time) whenever they need funds, **without breaking the entire deposit and without losing high interest on the remaining deposit**.
*   **Adjusted Balance:** The principal value of the Term Deposit Receipt is reduced accordingly, and the remaining balance continues to fetch the high contracted interest rate. No penalty is levied on such part withdrawals.
*   **Limits:** Minimum deposit ₹10,000; maximum ₹10 Lakhs.
*   **Tenure range:** 46 days to 120 months.

### 📌 B. PNB Sugam Plus (444 Days Promotional Scheme)
PNB's premier mid-term high-yield fixed deposit.
*   **Tenure:** Exactly 444 days.
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹10,000 (and in multiples of ₹1,000 thereafter); maximum ₹10 Crore.
*   **Interest Rates:** Peak promotional rates of **6.60%** (General) | **7.10%** (Senior) | **7.40%** (Super Senior).
*   **Partial Withdrawal:** Note that partial withdrawals are *not allowed* under the Sugam Plus promotional variant.
*   **Default Auto-Renewal:** Set automatically for 12 months in the absence of instructions.

### 📌 C. PNB Tax Saver Fixed Deposit (Tax Shield)
Designed for tax-deductible savings.
*   **Tenure:** Exactly 5 Years (lock-in).
*   **Deposits:** Minimum ₹100; maximum ₹1.5 Lakhs per financial year.
*   **Rules:** No premature cancellation or loan facility is permitted during the lock-in period.

### 📌 D. PNB Multi-Benefit Term Deposit Scheme
A traditional reinvestment fixed deposit plan designed to maximize compounding returns over the long term.
*   **Compounding:** Interest is compounded quarterly, increasing the principal balance, and the cumulative sum is paid as a lump-sum at maturity.
*   **Overdraft Facility:** Available up to 90% of the accumulated balance.

### 📌 E. PNB Special Recurring Deposit Product
An institutional systematic savings plan.
*   **Eligibility:** Government or private institutions, corporate bodies, and PSUs.
*   **Rule:** Can open recurring deposits in the name of the institution A/C employee. Requires a minimum of 10 employees or 75% of eligible employees, whichever is lower, to establish the disciplined group savings pool.

## 3. Recurring Deposit (RD) Scheme
PNB's systematic monthly installment savings program is designed as follows:

*   **Tenure Range:** 6 months to 120 months (10 years) in multiples of 1 month.
*   **Installment Limits:** Minimum entry of **₹100 per month**, and thereafter in multiples of ₹1. Maximum monthly installment of **₹25 Lakhs** per customer.
*   **Interest Rates:** Card rates as displayed in the table above, compounded quarterly.
*   **Delayed Installment Policy:** Delayed installments can be deposited before maturity by paying a nominal penalty of **₹1 per ₹100 per month** overdue.
*   **Premature Withdrawal Penalty:** Early closure is allowed but incurs a **1.00% penalty** deducted from the applicable card rate.
*   **Loan Against Deposit:** Avail of demand loans or overdrafts up to **90% of the accumulated RD balance**.
