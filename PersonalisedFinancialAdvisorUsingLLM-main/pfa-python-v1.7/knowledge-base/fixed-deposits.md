# Fixed Deposits (FDs)

**What it is:** A lump sum deposited with a bank or NBFC for a fixed tenure at a fixed interest rate.

**Typical returns:** 6-7.5% annually in India for tenures of 1-5 years (rates vary by bank and tenure; always check current rates via the live market data tool rather than assuming these numbers are current).

**Liquidity:** Low-medium. Premature withdrawal usually incurs a penalty (typically 0.5-1% reduction in interest rate).

**Risk:** Very low. Bank FDs are insured up to a limit (e.g. DICGC insures up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank in India).

**Taxation:** Interest is fully taxable as per the investor's income slab. No indexation benefit. TDS applies above a threshold.

**Best for:** Emergency funds, short-term goals (under 3 years), capital preservation, conservative investors, senior citizens (who often get a rate premium).

**Not ideal for:** Long-term wealth building, since post-tax post-inflation real returns are often close to zero or negative.
