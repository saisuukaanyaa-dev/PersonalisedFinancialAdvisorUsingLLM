# ETFs and Index Funds

**What it is:** A basket of securities (stocks, bonds, or commodities) that tracks an index, traded on an exchange (ETF) or bought/sold at end-of-day NAV (index mutual fund).

**Typical returns:** Historically 10-14% CAGR over long periods (10+ years) for broad equity index ETFs, though returns are volatile year to year and not guaranteed. Always verify current NAV and historical CAGR via the live market data tool.

**Liquidity:** High for ETFs (traded like stocks during market hours). Index funds settle in 1-3 days.

**Risk:** Medium to high, depends on the underlying index. Broad market index ETFs are lower risk than sector-specific or thematic ETFs.

**Taxation (India, equity-oriented):** LTCG (holdings over 1 year) taxed at a preferential rate above an exemption threshold; STCG (under 1 year) taxed at a higher flat rate. Debt ETFs follow debt taxation rules. Tax rules change periodically — verify against current rules before advising.

**Best for:** Long-term goals (5+ years), investors wanting low-cost diversified equity exposure, core portfolio holdings.

**Expense ratio:** Typically much lower than actively managed mutual funds (often under 0.3% for broad index ETFs vs 1-2%+ for active funds), which compounds meaningfully over long horizons.
