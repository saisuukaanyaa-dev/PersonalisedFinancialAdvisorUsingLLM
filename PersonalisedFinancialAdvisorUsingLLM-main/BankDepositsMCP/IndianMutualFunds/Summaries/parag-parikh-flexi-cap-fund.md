# Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund (Direct Growth)

## 📌 Executive Summary
**Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund** is one of India's premier flexi-cap equity schemes managed by **PPFAS Mutual Fund**. It is designed for long-term capital appreciation by investing dynamically across Indian large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap stocks, along with selective allocation to international equities (up to 35%).
