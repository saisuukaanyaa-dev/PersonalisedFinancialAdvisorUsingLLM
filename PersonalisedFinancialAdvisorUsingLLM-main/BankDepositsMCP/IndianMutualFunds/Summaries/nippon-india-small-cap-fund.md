# Nippon India Small Cap Fund (Direct Growth)

## 📌 Executive Summary
**Nippon India Small Cap Fund** is India's largest small-cap equity scheme managed by **Nippon India Mutual Fund**. It aims to generate long-term capital growth by predominantly investing in high-potential small-cap companies with scalable business models and high growth trajectories.

---

- **Category**: Equity — Small Cap
- **Fund House (AMC)**: Nippon India Mutual Fund
- **Assets Under Management (AUM)**: ₹82,580 Crore
- **Expense Ratio**: 0.70%
- **Risk Level**: Very High

## 🎯 Key Features & Strategy
- **Broad Portfolio Diversification**: Holds a diversified basket of over 100 small-cap stocks to mitigate individual company risk.
- **Tactical Large/Mid-Cap Overlay**: Maintains tactical allocations to larger companies to provide liquidity and manage risk during market downturns.
