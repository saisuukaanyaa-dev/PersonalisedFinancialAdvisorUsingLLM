# HDFC Flexi Cap Fund (Direct Growth)

## 📌 Executive Summary
**HDFC Flexi Cap Fund** is a flagship flexi-cap equity scheme offered by **HDFC Mutual Fund**. With over two decades of operational history, the fund invests across companies of varying market capitalizations, shifting weightings between large, mid, and small caps based on prevailing market valuations and macroeconomic conditions.
