## 2. Comprehensive Breakdown of Individual Schemes

### 📐 A. Digital Fixed Deposit (VKYC Instant Booking)
A seamless, paperwork-free term deposit designed for digital-native customers.
*   **How it works:** Non-Axis customers can open a high-interest fixed deposit instantly using Video KYC (VKYC) without having to open a regular savings account.
*   **Rates:** Entitles the saver to the same premium rates as physical Axis FD accounts, fully managed online via the Axis Mobile App.

### 📐 B. Fixed Deposit Plus (Non-Callable Bulk Deposit)
Designed for high-net-worth retail individuals looking to lock in peak market interest rates.
*   **Minimum Deposit:** **₹3 Crore**.
*   **Rule:** Premature withdrawal or partial breaks are strictly prohibited.
*   **Interest Advantage:** Offers a guaranteed rate premium of 10 bps to 25 bps above standard callable card rates across similar tenures.

### 📐 C. Auto Fixed Deposit (Sweep-In Facility)
Provides the ultimate balance between high interest earnings and immediate transactional liquidity.
*   **How it works:** Links your savings account to a dynamic fixed deposit. Any funds in the savings account exceeding a specified limit (e.g., ₹25,000) are automatically swept into a Fixed Deposit in units of **₹10,000** to earn higher interest.
*   **Liquidity:** If the savings account balance falls, units of the FD are automatically swept back in multiples of **₹1,000** to prevent check bounces or failed transactions without incurring overdraft penalties.

### 📐 D. Tax Saver Fixed Deposit
A traditional tax-saving term deposit.
*   **Tenure:** Exactly 5 Years (lock-in).
*   **Limits:** Min deposit ₹100; max ₹1.5 Lakhs per financial year. Offers a rate of **7.00% p.a.** (General) | **7.75% p.a.** (Seniors).

### 📐 E. FD-Linked Credit Cards (Credit Against Deposit)
Provides credit access to individuals who may not have traditional income proofs (e.g. students, home-makers, freelancers).
*   **How it works:** Axis Bank issues credit cards like *My Zone Easy*, *IndianOil Easy*, or *Privilege Easy* with the depositor's Fixed Deposit acting as collateral.
*   **Credit Limit:** Up to **80% of the FD principal value**. No credit score check or income document is required.

### 📐 F. Overdraft / Loan Against Fixed Deposit
Avail of instant funding to meet emergency expenses without liquidating your deposit.
*   **Limit:** Up to **85% - 90% of the FD principal**.
*   **Interest Cost:** Calculated only on the utilized cash balance, priced at a nominal spread of **1% to 2% over the FD interest rate**.

## 3. Standard Recurring Deposit (RD) Scheme
Axis Bank's systematic savings RD compounding program is structured as follows:

*   **Tenure Range:** 6 months to 120 months (10 years) in multiples of 3 months.
*   **Installment Limits:** Minimum **₹1,000 monthly**, and in multiples of ₹100 thereafter.
*   **Interest Rates (Compounded Quarterly):**
    *   **6 Months:** **5.50% p.a.** (General) | **6.00% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **9 Months:** **5.75% p.a.** (General) | **6.25% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **12 Months:** **6.25% p.a.** (General) | **6.75% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **15 Months:** **6.45% p.a.** (General) | **6.95% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **18 to 36 Months:** **6.50% p.a.** (General) | **7.00% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **60 to 120 Months:** **6.50% p.a.** (General) | **7.25% p.a.** (Senior)
*   **Late Installment Penalty:** If opened on or after August 9, 2016, a late fee of **₹10 for every ₹1,000 overdue** is levied monthly.
*   **Premature Withdrawal Penalty:** If the deposit is closed prematurely, interest is paid at **1% below the applicable card rate** for the period the deposit actually remained with the bank (applicable to deposits under ₹5 Crore).
