## 2. Comprehensive Breakdown of Individual Schemes

### 💎 A. IDFC FIRST Recurring Deposit with Zero Installment Penalty
A revolutionary systematic savings product designed to accommodate life's emergencies without penalty.
*   **Core Feature:** IDFC FIRST Bank **does not charge any penalty for missed or delayed recurring deposit installments**. Depositors can skip installments without losing accumulated yields.
*   **Minimum Deposit:** Start saving with just **₹100 per month**.
*   **Tenures:** Flexible options of **6, 9, 12, 24, and 36 months**.
*   **Compounding:** Interest is compounded quarterly.

### 💎 B. Senior Citizen Term Deposit (Premature Penalty Exempt)
A heavily incentivized fixed-income program for retirees.
*   **Rate Premium:** Offers an additional **50 bps premium** on standard term deposits.
*   **Complete Penalty Exemption:** Senior citizens are **completely exempt from the standard 1.00% premature closure penalty** on fixed and recurring deposits. They can break deposits early without penalty.

### 💎 C. Standard Fixed Deposit (Cumulative & Non-Cumulative)
Core fixed deposit product providing quarterly compounding.
*   **Compounding:** Quarterly compounded reinvestment plan, or monthly discounted payout.
*   **Premature Withdrawal Rules:** Callable deposits have a standard **1.00% premature closure penalty** for general public (waived upon death of holder, and for government or quasi-government bodies).

### 💎 D. IDFC FIRST Tax Saver Fixed Deposit
Provides tax deductions under Section 80C. Attracts a 5-year lock-in period with a maximum deposit cap of ₹1.5 Lakhs.
