## 2. Comprehensive Breakdown of Individual Schemes

### 🔑 A. ActiveMoney (Dynamic Sweep-In Program)
A revolutionary liquid-to-yield automation sweep-in service designed to maximize interest earnings without locking funds in.
*   **How it works:** By activating ActiveMoney, whenever your Kotak Savings Account balance exceeds **₹25,000**, the surplus is automatically swept into a **180-day Fixed Deposit** in multiples of ₹10,000.
*   **Interest Yield:** The swept surplus immediately earns a high term deposit interest rate of up to **7.00% p.a.**
*   **Liquidity:** If you need to make a transaction or withdraw cash and your savings account balance falls, the swept FD is automatically broken in multiples of ₹10,000 to cover the transaction without any premature withdrawal penalty.

### 🔑 B. Tax Saving Fixed Deposit
A dedicated tax saving fixed deposit scheme.
*   **Tenure:** Exactly 5 Years (lock-in).
*   **Deposits:** From ₹100 up to ₹1.5 Lakhs per financial year under Section 80C.
*   **Options:** Interest can be paid out quarterly, monthly, or accumulated till maturity.

### 🔑 C. Senior Citizen Fixed Deposit
*   **Core Benefit:** Kotak Mahindra Bank offers senior citizens aged 60 years and above an additional **50 bps premium** across all tenures under ₹3 Crore.
*   **Rates:** Reaches up to **7.60% p.a.** on standard 1 to 2-year tenures, representing one of the highest private sector yields in India.

## 3. Recurring Deposit (RD) Scheme
Kotak's disciplined monthly systematic investment plan is structured as follows:

*   **Tenure Range:** 6 months to 120 months (10 years) in multiples of 3 months.
*   **Installment Limits:** Starts with a low minimum of **₹100 per month**.
*   **Maximum Deposit Caps:**
    *   *Tenures up to 36 months:* Restricted to **₹1,00,000 per month**.
    *   *Tenures greater than 36 months:* Restricted to **₹25,000 per month**.
*   **Compounding:** Interest is compounded quarterly.
*   **Late Payout Penalty:** Missing or delaying a monthly RD installment attracts a penalty fee calculated at **2.00% per annum** on the overdue installment for the period of delay.
*   **Account Pre-requisite:** It is mandatory to hold an active savings account with Kotak Mahindra Bank to set up the auto-debit systematic recurring deposit.
