## 2. Comprehensive Breakdown of Individual Schemes

### 🦁 A. ICICI Bank Golden Years FD
A highly celebrated promotional scheme engineered to provide maximized interest security for senior citizens on long-term assets.
*   **Tenure:** 5 years 1 day to 10 years.
*   **Rate Premium:** Offers an additional **80 bps premium** (comprising 50 bps standard senior citizen premium + an extra 30 bps promotional premium) over standard general rates, yielding **7.40% p.a.** (standard card yields 6.55% p.a. on selected long-term brackets).
*   **Maturity options:** Both cumulative compounding and monthly/quarterly payout models are supported.

### 🦁 B. iWish Deposits (Goal-Based Flexible RD)
A revolutionary flexible savings account designed to remove the stress of rigid recurring deposit timelines.
*   **How it works:** Unlike a standard RD where missing a payment leads to financial penalties, iWish allows depositors to skip monthly payments completely without penalty.
*   **Top-Ups:** Depositors can deposit variable amounts multiple times a month whenever they have surplus cash.
*   **Interest Yield:** Offers high rates up to **7.10% p.a.** equivalent to standard term deposit rates.
*   **Minimum Goal:** Start with a target of ₹5,000 with tenures from 6 to 60 months.

### 🦁 C. Delightful Deposits (Co-Branded Systematic Savings)
Special recurring deposit plans executed in collaboration with premier commercial partners (Croma, MakeMyTrip, Tanishq, Thomas Cook).
*   **Feature:** Upon maturity, the accumulated principal and interest are converted into brand-specific gift cards or vouchers with an added cash multiplier or discounts (e.g., getting an extra 5% to 10% value on Tanishq jewelry purchases).
*   **Booking Channels:** Fully digital execution via the iMobile Pay app or NetBanking.

### 🦁 D. Monthly Pay-Out Fixed Deposit
Tailored specifically for depositors who rely on fixed deposits for monthly cash flow (e.g. retirees).
*   **Feature:** Calculates quarterly compounded interest but discounts it to pay out an equal monthly income stream directly to the linked savings account.

### 🦁 E. Overdraft against Fixed Deposit (Digital FD Line)
Allows depositors to instantly generate a line of credit against their FD through the iMobile app.
*   **Limit:** Up to **90% of the FD value**.
*   **Advantage:** Zero processing fees and zero paperwork; interest is charged at 1.00% to 1.50% above the FD rate on the daily utilized balance only.

## 3. Standard Recurring Deposit (RD) Scheme
Provides steady, structured wealth accumulation with systematic monthly installments.

*   **Tenure Range:** 6 months to 10 years (in multiples of 3 months).
*   **Installment Limits:** Starts from a low entry threshold of **₹500 monthly**, and in multiples of ₹100 thereafter.
*   **Interest Rates (Compounded Quarterly):**
    *   **Upto 6 Months:** **4.50% p.a.** (General) | **5.00% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **6 to 9 Months:** **5.50% p.a.** (General) | **6.00% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **9 to 15 Months:** **6.25% p.a.** (General) | **6.75% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **15 to 24 Months:** **6.30% p.a.** (General) | **6.80% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **2 to 3 Years:** **6.45% p.a.** (General) | **6.95% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **3 to 5 Years:** **6.50% p.a.** (General) | **7.10% p.a.** (Senior)
*   **Penalty for Late Installments:** Strict late fee of **₹12 per ₹1,000 per month** overdue. Part of a month is rounded up as a full month for penalty deduction, which is adjusted from total interest at maturity.
*   **Premature Withdrawal Rules:** Early closure is allowed. No interest is payable for accounts closed within 7 days. Beyond 7 days, a premature withdrawal penalty is levied as follows:
    *   *Tenure < 1 Year:* **0.50% penal deduction**.
    *   *Tenure 1 Year to < 5 Years:* **1.00% penal deduction**.
    *   *Tenure 5 Years and above:* **1.00% penal deduction** (under ₹5 Crore).
