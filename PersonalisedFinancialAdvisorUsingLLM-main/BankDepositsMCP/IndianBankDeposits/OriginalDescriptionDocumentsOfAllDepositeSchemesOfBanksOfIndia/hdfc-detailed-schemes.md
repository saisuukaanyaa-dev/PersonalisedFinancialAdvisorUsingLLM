# HDFC Bank Detailed Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

HDFC Bank, India's largest private sector bank, offers structured and highly competitive retail deposit options, combining secure wealth growth with unique auxiliary benefits like health cover.

## 1. Fixed Deposit (FD) Schemes

### Standard Domestic Fixed Deposits
*   **Yield Range:** Standard interest rates range from **2.75% to 6.50% p.a.** for the general public, and **3.25% to 7.00% p.a.** for senior citizens.
*   **Peak/Promotional Rates:** HDFC Bank offers its peak rates of **7.20% p.a.** (general) and **7.70% p.a.** (senior citizens) on selective, structured tenures such as its **35-month and 55-month FD** brackets.

### HDFC Bank HealthCover FD
*   **Concept:** A unique hybrid deposit product that bridges wealth accumulation and health security.
*   **Key Feature:** Combines a regular fixed deposit with a critical illness insurance policy, providing medical coverage alongside interest compounding. It allows retail depositors to secure guaranteed returns while ensuring cash availability and health protection.

### Five Year Tax Saving Fixed Deposit
*   **Lock-in Period:** Strict **5-year** statutory lock-in period with no premature withdrawals or loans against the deposit permitted.
*   **Tax Benefit:** Eligible for tax deductions up to ₹1.5 Lakhs under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.

### Non-Withdrawable Deposits
*   **Eligibility:** Typically offered for bulk deposits or specific retail brackets where the depositor explicitly foregoes the premature withdrawal option.
*   **Benefit:** Offers preferential, higher interest yields over standard callable FDs in exchange for zero liquidity before maturity.

## 2. Recurring Deposit (RD) Schemes

HDFC Bank recurring deposits offer systematic monthly investment paths matching the yields of standard term deposits.

*   **Tenure Range:** Highly flexible options starting from **6 months up to 120 months** (10 years).
*   **RD Yield Structure:**
    *   **1 Year to 2 Years:** **6.60% p.a.** (General) | **7.10% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **2 Years to 3 Years:** **7.10% p.a.** (General) | **7.60% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **Standard RD Range:** Interest rates scale from **4.50% to 7.25% p.a.** for the general public, and up to **7.75% p.a.** for senior citizens depending on the selected tenure.
*   **Minimum Investment:** Standard minimum deposit of **₹1,000 monthly**, and thereafter in multiples of ₹100.

### My Passion Fund
*   **Concept:** A modern, highly flexible goal-based systematic savings variant of the traditional recurring deposit.
*   **Key Feature:** Enables depositors to build a custom fund for specific lifestyle or financial milestones. Unlike standard rigid RDs, it allows users to add subsequent, flexible deposits into their goal fund whenever they have surplus cash, reinforcing positive, passionate savings habits.
