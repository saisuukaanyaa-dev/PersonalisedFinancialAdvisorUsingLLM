# IndusInd Bank Detailed Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

IndusInd Bank, a prominent private sector innovator, provides market-leading interest rate steps on short-to-medium-term retail placements and systematic savings accounts that bundle attractive lifestyle privileges.

## 1. Standard Term Deposits (Premature Withdrawal Allowed)
IndusInd Bank offers competitive, multi-tiered yield steps on retail domestic placements below ₹3 Crore, allowing investors to maximize interest on short-to-medium-term liquidity.

### A. Yield Structure & Policy Rules
*   **Peak General Yields:** Reaches **7.00% p.a.** on maturity tenures of 2 Years to 3 Years (w.e.f. July 24, 2026).
*   **Senior Citizen Bonus:** Resident seniors receive an additional **75 bps** across all maturity brackets, elevating peak yields to **7.75% p.a.** on 2 to 3-year tenures. *(Senior citizen rates are not applicable to NRE/NRO accounts).*
*   **Interest Payout Flexibility:** Option of monthly discounted payouts, quarterly cash payouts, half-yearly payouts, or cumulative compounding at quarterly rests.
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹10,000 for standard accounts; lower limits for digital bookings.

## 2. Value Added Recurring Deposits
IndusInd Bank’s Value Added Recurring Deposits are unique systematic investment plans that combine traditional savings yields with practical lifestyle, shopping, and milestone rewards.

### A. Key Specifications
*   **Minimum Monthly Investment:** ₹500.
*   **Tenure Range:** 12 months to 120 months.
*   **Peak Interest Rates:** Up to **7.50% p.a.** for general depositors, and **8.25% p.a.** for senior citizens.
*   **Special Lifestyle Features:** 
    *   Integrates milestone-based rewards (e.g., reward points, shopping vouchers, or travel discounts) with top retail partners as deposits grow.
    *   Enables automated standing instructions from any linked savings account.
*   **Overdue Installments:** Standard private sector late-payment charges are calculated based on the delayed installment amount and overdue period.

---

## 3. Premature Closure & Partial Breakage Rules
IndusInd Bank permits premature withdrawal and partial breakage of term deposits under the following guidelines:
*   **Early Withdrawal Penalty:** A premature closure penalty of **0.50% to 1.00%** is deducted from the applicable interest rate.
*   **Interest Pay-out Adjustment:** Interest is paid at the rate applicable on the date of deposit for the actual period the deposit remained with the bank, minus the penalty percentage.
*   **No-Interest Lock-in:** No interest is payable if the deposit is prematurely withdrawn before completing the minimum term of **7 days** (or 1 year for NRE deposits).
