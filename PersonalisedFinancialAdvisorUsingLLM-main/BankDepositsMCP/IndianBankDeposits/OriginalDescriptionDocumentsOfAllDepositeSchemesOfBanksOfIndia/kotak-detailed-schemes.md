# Kotak Mahindra Bank Detailed Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

Kotak Mahindra Bank offers competitive interest rates on retail domestic term deposits, systematic recurring options, and highly innovative auto-sweeping programs.

## 1. Fixed Deposit (FD) Portfolio

Kotak Mahindra Bank provides multiple high-yield lump-sum investment options:

### A. Standard Retail Fixed Deposits
*   **Tenure Range**: 7 days to 10 years (120 months).
*   **Interest Rates**: Standard rates range from **2.75% to 6.80% p.a.** for the general public, and **3.25% to 7.30% p.a.** for resident senior citizens.
*   **Peak Rates**: Offered on medium-term tenures (specifically between 1 Year and 2 Years) reaching up to **7.30% p.a.** for senior citizens.
*   **Minimum Deposit**: ₹5,000 for standard accounts opened offline, with digital-first FDs allowed at lower thresholds.
*   **Compounding**: Compounded quarterly for tenures exceeding 180 days.

### B. ActiveMoney (Auto-Sweep Facility)
*   **The Problem**: Idle savings accounts earn low interest (typically 3.0% to 3.5%), but FDs lack instant transactional liquidity.
*   **ActiveMoney Solution**: 
    *   **Automation**: Whenever the savings account balance exceeds a threshold of **₹25,000**, the excess funds are automatically swept into a **180-day Fixed Deposit** in multiples of ₹10,000.
    *   **High Yield**: The swept funds earn the 180-day FD interest rate (which is significantly higher than the standard savings rate).
    *   **Zero-Penalty Sweep-Back**: If the user makes a transaction, writes a cheque, or withdraws cash exceeding their savings balance, the bank automatically pulls money back from the swept FDs (on a LIFO - Last In First Out basis) with **zero premature withdrawal penalties**.

### C. Tax Saving Fixed Deposit
*   **Lock-in Period**: Mandated **5-year** lock-in. No premature withdrawal, partial breakage, or loan-against-deposit facility is allowed.
*   **Tax Benefits**: Eligible for tax exemption up to ₹1.5 Lakhs per financial year under Section 80C.
*   **Deposit Limits**: Minimum of ₹100 and a maximum of ₹1.5 Lakhs per financial year.

## 2. Recurring Deposit (RD) Portfolio

Recurring Deposits (RDs) are structured to build a disciplined savings habit over a fixed term.

### B. Standard Recurring Deposits
*   **Tenure Range**: Minimum **6 months** up to **120 months (10 years)** (in multiples of 3 months).
*   **Minimum Deposit**: **₹100 per month** (can be increased in multiples of ₹100 thereafter).
*   **Maximum Monthly Deposit**: Standard limits cap certain long-term tenures (above 36 months) to ₹25,000 per month, while shorter terms are allowed up to ₹1,00,000 per month without prior branch approvals.
*   **Interest Rates**: Aligned closely with Fixed Deposit card rates across identical tenures, reaching up to **7.10% p.a.** for general citizens and **7.60% p.a.** for senior citizens in the 1-to-2-year bracket.

## 3. Regulatory Penalties, TDS & Operational Terms

### A. Late Payment Penalty on RD Installments
*   **Rule**: If a depositor fails to pay the monthly installment within the grace window, an overdue penalty applies.
*   **Calculation**: Charged at **2.00% per annum** for the overdue period on the installment amount. This penalty is calculated monthly and deducted from the cumulative interest at the time of maturity.

### B. Premature Withdrawal Penalties
*   **Standard FDs/RDs**: A premature closure or partial break of a callable deposit attracts an interest rate markdown.
*   **Penal Interest**: Typically, the bank penalizes premature breakage by charging **0.50% to 1.00%** below the rate applicable for the period the deposit actually ran or below the contracted rate, whichever is lower.
*   **Senior Citizen Protections**: Preferential terms may protect senior citizen deposits from maximum markdown limits on specific domestic terms.

### C. TDS & Tax Guidelines
*   **TDS Threshold**: Applicable on total interest earned across all branches of the bank exceeding **₹40,000** (regular) and **₹50,000** (senior citizens) in a financial year.
*   **PAN Submission**: Mandatory to provide PAN. Form 15G (for general citizens with zero tax liability) or Form 15H (for senior citizens) can be submitted to prevent TDS deduction.
