# Axis Bank Detailed Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

Axis Bank offers a broad suite of retail fixed deposits, recurring deposits, and automated liquidity sweeps designed to match different investment horizons and risk appetites.

## 1. Fixed Deposit (FD) Portfolio

Axis Bank offers several options for lump-sum deposits:

### A. Standard Retail Fixed Deposits (Domestic)
*   **Tenure Range**: 7 days to 10 years.
*   **Interest Rates**: Range from **3.00% to 6.45% p.a.** for general citizens, and **3.50% to 7.20% p.a.** for senior citizens.
*   **Promotional Buckets**: Selective medium-term tenures offer peak rates of up to **7.20% p.a.** for senior citizens.
*   **Minimum Deposit**: Typically starts from ₹5,000 for standard retail accounts (online digital FDs can start lower).
*   **Compounding**: Compounded quarterly for tenures exceeding 180 days.

### B. Fixed Deposit Plus (Non-Callable)
*   **Target Segment**: High net-worth individuals and corporate deposits.
*   **Minimum Deposit Value**: **₹3 Crore**.
*   **Core Rule**: No premature withdrawal is permitted under any circumstances for retail or bulk non-callable deposits, in exchange for higher differential yields (typically 10 to 30 bps premium over callable standard FDs).
*   **Availability**: Offered across all domestic branches and NRI NRE/NRO categories.

### C. Tax Saver Fixed Deposit
*   **Lock-in Period**: Strict **5-year** lock-in. No premature withdrawal or loan/overdraft facilities are allowed during this period.
*   **Tax Benefit**: Eligible for deductions up to ₹1.5 Lakhs per financial year under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.
*   **Interest Payout Options**: Can be cumulative (reinvestment) or quarterly/monthly payout.

### D. Auto Fixed Deposit (Auto-Sweep / Sweep-In)
*   **Mechanics**: Links a savings or current account to an automated FD. Balances above a pre-designated threshold are automatically swept into an FD in multiples of ₹1,000 or ₹10,000.
*   **Liquidity**: If the liquid account experiences a deficit, the FD is automatically swept back (broken in reverse-chronological order or LIFO) to clear payments, minimizing loss of interest.

## 2. Recurring Deposit (RD) Portfolio & Systematic Plans

Recurring Deposits allow depositors to build disciplined savings incrementally.

### A. Standard Recurring Deposits
*   **Tenure Range**: Minimum **6 months** up to a maximum of **120 months (10 years)** in multiples of 3 months.
*   **Minimum Monthly Installment**: **₹500**.
*   **Interest Rates (Less than ₹3 Crore)**:
    *   *6 Months*: **5.50% p.a.** (General) | **6.00% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   *9 Months*: **5.75% p.a.** (General) | **6.25% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   *12 Months*: **6.25% p.a.** (General) | **6.75% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   *15 Months*: **6.45% p.a.** (General) | **6.95% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   *18 to 48 Months*: **6.50% p.a.** (General) | **7.00% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   *60 to 120 Months*: **6.50% p.a.** (General) | **7.25% p.a.** (Senior)

### B. Dream Builder RD
*   **Concept**: A goal-based systematic savings product allowing depositors to automate monthly transfers toward specific lifestyle, travel, or emergency goals, utilizing the bank's digital calculators and automated SIP frameworks.

## 3. Penalty Calculations, TDS & Ancillary Benefits

### A. Late Payment Penalty for RDs
*   **Rule (w.e.f. August 9, 2016)**: If a monthly installment is missed or delayed, a late fee is levied.
*   **Calculation**: **₹10 for every ₹1,000** overdue per month.
*   **Compounding of Delays**: Part of a month is treated as a full month for penalty calculations.

### B. Premature Withdrawal Penalty
*   **Rule**: Applicable on early closures of deposits below ₹5 Crore opened or renewed on or after May 1, 2014.
*   **Calculation**: Interest is paid at **1.00% below the contracted rate** or the card rate applicable for the period the deposit actually remained with the bank, whichever is lower.

### C. FD-Linked Credit Cards
*   **Concept**: Allows customers (including those with no credit history) to get secure credit access by pledging an FD as collateral.
*   **Eligible Cards**: My Zone Easy, Privilege Easy, and IndianOil Easy Credit Cards.
*   **Credit Limit**: Typically issued at up to **80% to 90%** of the FD principal value, with the FD earning compounding interest simultaneously.

### D. TDS and Tax Guidelines
*   **TDS Threshold**: Imposed if aggregate interest across all branches exceeds **₹40,000** per financial year for regular citizens, or **₹50,000** for senior citizens.
*   **PAN Requirement**: Providing PAN ensures TDS deduction at a flat **10%**; otherwise, a non-PAN penalty of **20%** applies.
