# State Bank of India (SBI) - Original Scheme Description Documents

This document provides the consolidated, detailed specifications, rules, and terms-and-conditions for the complete portfolio of special and standard deposit schemes offered by the State Bank of India (SBI). 

---

## 1. Amrit Vrishti Deposit Scheme (444 Days)

### Description & Objectives
The **Amrit Vrishti** scheme is a premier, high-yield retail term deposit designed to close the gap between deposit growth and loan credit expansion. It is a specific-tenor product launched to provide individuals and senior citizens with highly competitive rates for a fixed lock-in period of 444 days.

### Key Specifications
*   **Eligible Customers:** Domestic retail depositors, including individuals, joint accounts, minors (with guardians), and HUFs. Also applicable to NRE and NRO retail accounts.
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹1,000.
*   **Maximum Deposit:** Below ₹3 Crore (Retail segment).
*   **Tenure:** Exactly 444 Days.
*   **Availability:** Open for fresh subscriptions and renewals until March 31, 2025.

### Interest Rate Structure
*   **General Public:** Baseline rate of **6.45% p.a.** (w.e.f. December 15, 2025). 
*   **Senior Citizens:** Offered an additional **50 bps premium**, resulting in **6.95% p.a.**
*   **Super Senior Citizens (80+):** Eligible for an additional **10 bps premium** over the senior citizen rate (under the *SBI Patrons* framework), yielding **7.05% p.a.**
*   *Note on Historical Rates:* Peak rates under the scheme reached up to **7.25% p.a.** for general depositors, **7.75% p.a.** for seniors, and **7.85% p.a.** for super seniors during peak liquidity cycles.

### Premature Withdrawal & Penalties
*   Premature withdrawal is allowed.
*   **Penalty:** Subject to standard retail term deposit penalties:
    *   For deposits up to ₹5 Lakhs: Penalty of **0.50%** below the rate applicable on the date of deposit for the period the deposit has remained with the bank, or the contracted rate, whichever is lower.
    *   For deposits above ₹5 Lakhs but below ₹3 Crore: Penalty of **1.00%** below the rate applicable for the period run or the contracted rate, whichever is lower.
    *   No interest is payable if closed before completing 7 days.

## 2. SBI We-Care Senior Citizen Deposit Scheme

### Description & Objectives
The **SBI We-Care** scheme is a dedicated term deposit designed to protect the interest income of senior citizens during periods of declining interest rates by offering a higher premium on long-term investments.

### Key Specifications
*   **Eligible Customers:** Resident Senior Citizens of age 60 years and above.
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹10,000.
*   **Maximum Deposit:** Below ₹3 Crore.
*   **Tenure:** Minimum 5 Years to a Maximum of 10 Years.

### Interest Rate Structure
*   **Premium Over Standard Card Rates:** Offers an additional premium of **50 bps** over and above the regular senior citizen interest rate (which is already 50 bps above the general public card rate).
*   **Total Premium:** **100 bps (1.00% p.a.)** higher than the general public card rate.
*   **Current Effective Yield:** **7.05% p.a.** (standard card rate of 6.05% + 1.00% total premium w.e.f. December 15, 2025).

### Premature Withdrawal & Special Clauses
*   Premature withdrawal is permitted.
*   **Penalty:** If closed prematurely, the special *We-Care* premium of 50 bps will **not** be paid. The interest rate applicable will be the standard senior citizen rate for the period run minus the standard premature penalty (0.50% or 1.00% depending on the amount).

## 3. SBI Patrons Scheme (Super Senior Citizens)

### Description & Objectives
The **SBI Patrons** scheme is a premium welfare-focused benefit specifically tailored for super senior citizens (individuals aged 80 and above) to support their livelihood with enhanced monthly or quarterly interest earnings on liquid wealth.

### Key Specifications
*   **Eligible Customers:** Super Senior Citizens who have attained the age of **80 years and above**.
*   **Interest Premium:** Offers an additional **10 bps (0.10%)** interest premium over and above the applicable Senior Citizen rate across all eligible tenures.
*   **Eligible Products:** Standard Domestic Callable Retail Term Deposits.
*   **Ineligible Products:** This scheme is strictly **not** applicable to:
    *   Recurring Deposits (RDs)
    *   SBI Green Rupee Term Deposits (SGRTD)
    *   SBI Tax Savings Scheme 2006
    *   Multi Option Deposits (MODS)
    *   Capital Gains Accounts Scheme (Capgain)
    *   Non-Callable Term Deposits (such as *Sarvottam*)

## 4. Sarvottam Domestic Retail Term Deposit (Non-Callable)

### Description & Objectives
The **Sarvottam** scheme is a high-value, non-callable domestic term deposit designed for savers with large surplus cash pools. By foregoing the right to premature withdrawal, depositors are rewarded with significantly higher interest margins compared to callable cards.

### Key Specifications
*   **Deposit Range:** Above **₹1 Crore** up to less than **₹3 Crore** (Retail segment).
*   **Callable Status:** Strictly **Non-Callable**. Premature withdrawal or partial breakage is **not permitted** under any circumstances, except in extreme eventualities like death of the depositor or regulatory/court orders.
*   **Tenures Allowed:** Only two specific tenures: **1 Year** and **2 Years**.

### Interest Rate Structure
*   **1 Year Tenure:**
    *   General Public: **6.55% p.a.** (30 bps above standard card rate)
    *   Senior Citizens: **7.05% p.a.** (30 bps above standard senior rate)
*   **2 Year Tenure:**
    *   General Public: **6.80% p.a.** (40 bps above standard card rate)
    *   Senior Citizens: **7.30% p.a.** (40 bps above standard senior rate)

## 5. SBI Green Rupee Term Deposit (SGRTD)

### Description & Objectives
The **SBI Green Rupee Term Deposit (SGRTD)** is an eco-friendly investment scheme designed to mobilize financial resources for financing green, climate-resilient, and environmentally sustainable projects (e.g., solar, wind power, and waste management) in alignment with the RBI's green deposit framework.

### Key Specifications
*   **Eligible Customers:** Resident Individuals, Joint Holdings, Minors, and HUFs. Also open to Non-Resident Indians (NRE/NRO) and Corporates/Institutions.
*   **Tenures Allowed:** Three specific tenures:
    *   **1111 Days**
    *   **1777 Days**
    *   **2222 Days**
*   **Interest Rate Pricing:** Priced **at par with standard Domestic Retail Term Deposit card rates** (w.e.f. May 1, 2026). Earlier iterations offered these at 10 bps below card rates.
*   **Interest Payout Options:** Available under:
    *   *Cumulative Plan:* Compounded quarterly and paid on maturity.
    *   *Non-Cumulative Plan:* Paid at monthly, quarterly, or half-yearly intervals based on customer instructions.

## 6. Multi Option Deposit Scheme (MODS)

### Description & Objectives
The **Multi Option Deposit Scheme (MODS)** is a transactional, highly liquid term deposit linked directly to a customer's Savings Bank or Current Account. It offers an automatic sweeping mechanism that balances the high returns of a term deposit with the instant liquidity of a transactional account.

### Operational Mechanics & Rules
*   **Automatic Sweep-In:** When the linked savings or current account balance falls below a pre-determined threshold, funds are automatically swept in multiples of **₹1,000** from the MODS into the transactional account to prevent cheque bounces or transaction failures.
*   **Breaking the Deposit:** Unlike standard FDs where the entire receipt must be broken, MODS allows **partial breakout in units of ₹1,000**.
*   **Remaining Balance:** The remaining balance in the MODS continues to earn the originally contracted term deposit rate.
*   **Premature Penalty on Broken Portion:** Standard premature withdrawal penalties (0.50% or 1.00%) apply *only* to the units broken and swept back, calculated for the exact period they remained in the MODS. The untouched units continue compounding without penalty.
*   **Tenure:** 1 Year to 5 Years.
