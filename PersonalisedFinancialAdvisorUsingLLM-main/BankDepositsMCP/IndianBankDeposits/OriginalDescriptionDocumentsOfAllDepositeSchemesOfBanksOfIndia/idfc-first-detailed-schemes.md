# IDFC FIRST Bank Detailed Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

IDFC FIRST Bank distinguishes itself in the Indian banking landscape with highly customer-centric, investor-friendly deposit terms, featuring high yield steps on medium-term placements, zero-penalty recurring deposits, and extensive exemptions for senior citizens.

## 1. High-Yield Retail Term Deposits
IDFC FIRST Bank offers attractive, progressive retail term deposit rates below ₹3 Crore (w.e.f. September 1, 2026), prioritizing secure and compounding wealth generation.

### A. Yield Tiers & Compounding Mechanics
*   **Core Retail Rates:** Up to **7.10% p.a.** for standard depositors on medium-term buckets (such as 24 and 36 months).
*   **Senior Citizen Premium:** Seniors enjoy an additional **25 bps** over standard retail rates, yielding up to **7.35% p.a.**
*   **Compounding rests:** 
    *   Rates for tenures up to 180 days are calculated on a **simple interest** basis.
    *   Rates for tenures above 180 days are compounded **quarterly**.
    *   Monthly interest payout options are calculated by discounting the standard quarterly compounding rate.

---

## 2. Zero-Penalty Recurring Deposits (RDs)
IDFC FIRST Bank’s recurring deposit product is designed with a "zero-penalty" framework, offering maximum flexibility for disciplined savers.

### A. Key Specifications & Exclusives
*   **Minimum Deposit Amount:** Open an account with as low as **₹100 per month**.
*   **Tenure Range:** 6 months, 9 months, 12 months, 24 months, and 36 months.
*   **Core RD Interest Rates:** Matches standard compounding FD rates for identical tenures (up to **7.10% p.a.** general and **7.35% p.a.** for seniors).
*   **The No-Penalty Revolution:**
    *   **No Penalty on Missed Installments:** IDFC FIRST Bank understands that financial emergencies happen. The bank **does not charge any penalty** or late fee if you miss or delay your monthly recurring deposit installments.
    *   **Flexible Digital Management:** Depositors can easily pause, skip, or track their systematic savings online via the IDFC FIRST mobile banking application.

---

## 3. Premature Closure & Senior Citizen Exemptions
*   **Standard Premature Penalty:** A **1.00% penalty** is applicable on premature or partial withdrawals of fixed and recurring deposits opened on or after May 2, 2019.
*   **How Interest is Calculated:** Interest is paid at the rate applicable on the date of deposit for the period the deposit remained with the bank, or at the contracted rate (whichever is lower), minus the 1.00% penalty.
*   **Exclusions & Exemptions (Highly Investor-Friendly):**
    *   **Senior Citizen Exemption:** All Fixed Deposits and Recurring Deposits held by Senior Citizens (deposits below ₹3 Crore) are **completely exempt** from the 1.00% premature closure penalty.
    *   **Bereavement Exemption:** Premature closures effected due to the death of the primary account holder(s) are **completely exempt** from any premature closure penalty.
    *   **Government Exclusions:** Premature closure penalties are not levied on deposits of Government, Quasi-Government, Statutory, or Regulatory bodies.
*   **Lock-in Limits:** No interest is payable if domestic deposits are broken within **1 month** of booking (or within 1 year / 365 days for NRE accounts).
