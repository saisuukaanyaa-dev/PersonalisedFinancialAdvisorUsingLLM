# Canara Bank Detailed Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

Canara Bank, a leading public sector bank in India, provides a robust suite of retail term and recurring deposit products, including highly flexible and daily micro-savings schemes designed to support varied income patterns.

## 1. Kamadhenu Deposit (Reinvestment Plan)
The Kamadhenu Deposit is Canara Bank's premier cumulative term deposit scheme, where interest is compounded quarterly and paid along with the principal at maturity, maximizing compound growth.

### A. Key Specifications
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹1,000.
*   **Tenure Range:** 5 months to 120 months.
*   **Interest Compounding:** Compounded quarterly at the end of each financial quarter (June, September, December, and March) based on the daily balance.
*   **Target Audience:** Individuals, minors (through guardians), HUFs, partnership firms, companies, associations, and trusts.
*   **Loan/Overdraft Facility:** Available up to **90%** of the accrued deposit value, allowing liquidity without breaking the deposit.

## 2. Canara Dhanvarsha (Flexible Recurring Deposit)
Canara Dhanvarsha is a flexible recurring deposit (Flexi-RD) scheme specifically designed for depositors with fluctuating monthly incomes (such as freelancers or business owners), allowing them to deposit surplus funds whenever available.

### A. Key Specifications
*   **Minimum Monthly Installment:** ₹1,000 (and in multiples of ₹100 thereafter) or ₹500 depending on the specific account class.
*   **Maximum Monthly Deposit:** ₹1,00,000.
*   **Tenure Range:** 1 year to 10 years (in multiples of 3 months).
*   **Core Yield Tiers:** Aligned with standard card rates. Senior citizens receive an additional **50 bps** premium.
*   **Interactive Flexibility:** 
    *   Depositors can make **more than one installment** during any given month.
    *   Additional installments made during the month are **not** treated as prepayment of future mandatory monthly installments.
    *   There is **no penalty** for delayed payments or missed installments under this flexible systematic plan.
*   **Ancillary Benefits:** 
    *   Free standing instructions are available to auto-sweep funds from a linked Canara Bank savings account.
    *   Loan facility is available up to **90%** of the accumulated balance.
    *   **TDS Exemption:** No TDS is deducted if the interest remains below the statutory limits.

## 3. Nitya Nidhi Deposit (NND) Scheme
The Nitya Nidhi Deposit (NND) Scheme (which incorporates the merged Syndicate Bank Pigmy Deposit Scheme w.e.f. April 1, 2020) is a unique micro-savings product designed for daily wage earners, small traders, and doorstep savers.

### A. Operational Mechanics
*   **Doorstep Collection:** Canara Bank's authorized agents collect savings directly from the depositor’s doorstep at daily or convenient intervals.
*   **Deposit Limits:**
    *   *Minimum Monthly Contribution:* ₹50.
    *   *Daily Collection Ceiling:* Cannot exceed **₹1,000 per day** (maximum ₹30,000 in a month).
*   **Tenure:** Fixed at exactly **63 months** (5 years and 3 months).
*   **Maturity Rate of Interest:** **2.00% to 2.50% p.a.** on maturity.
*   **Loan Facility:** Up to **75%** of the outstanding balance.
*   **Premature Closure & Penalty Structure:**
    *   *Closed under 12 months:* Yields a revised rate of **0.10%** and incurs a penalty of **0.05%** on the interest payable. Alternatively, some terms levy a 3.00% principal deduction if closed prematurely before 1 year.
    *   *12 to 24 months:* Yields a revised rate of **0.25%** (no penalty).
    *   *24 to 39 months:* Yields a revised rate of **0.50%** (no penalty).
    *   *39 to 63 months:* Yields a revised rate of **1.00%** (no penalty).
    *   *At Maturity (63 months):* Full contracted rate of **2.00% - 2.50%** (no penalty).

---

## 4. Premature Withdrawal Policies
For all standard retail term deposits (including Kamadhenu and standard RDs) of less than ₹3 Crore opened or renewed:
*   **Standard Penalty:** A **1.00% penalty** is levied for premature closure, partial withdrawal, or premature extension.
*   **Rate Calculation:** Interest will be paid at **1.00% below the rate applicable** for the actual tenure the deposit ran, or 1.00% below the contracted rate, whichever is lower.
*   **Lock-in Grace:** No interest is paid on deposits closed within **7 days** of opening.
