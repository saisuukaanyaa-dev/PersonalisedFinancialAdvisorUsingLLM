# ICICI Bank Detailed Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

ICICI Bank is known for highly flexible online investment tools, digital recurring deposit programs (such as "iWish"), and competitive interest steps across standard and demographic-focused portfolios.

## 1. Fixed Deposit (FD) Schemes

### Standard Domestic Fixed Deposits
*   **Yield Range:** Standard interest rates range from **2.75% to 6.50% p.a.** for general citizens, and **3.25% to 7.10% p.a.** for senior citizens across tenures.

### ICICI Bank Golden Years FD
*   **Target Audience:** Tailored exclusively for resident Senior Citizens (60 years and above).
*   **Key Feature:** Offers an interest rate of **6.55% p.a.** for long-term tenures ranging from **5 years to 10 years**.
*   **Premium Advantage:** Provides an additional **80 bps** premium over the baseline standard interest rates applicable for identical long-term tenures, helping seniors secure optimal risk-free returns on their retirement corpus.

## 2. Recurring Deposit (RD) Schemes

ICICI Bank's recurring deposit portfolio stands out due to its rigid penalty structures for standard deposits and extreme flexibility options under its digital savings programs.

*   **Tenure Options:** Available from a minimum of **6 months up to a maximum of 10 years** (120 months), selectable in multiples of **3 months**.
*   **Minimum Entry Deposit:** Low threshold of **₹500 monthly**, with subsequent increments in multiples of ₹100.

### Standard RD Rate Card (w.e.f. December 29, 2025)
Interest is compounded quarterly and paid out upon maturity:

| Maturity Period | General Public (p.a.) | Senior Citizens (p.a.) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Upto 6 Months** | 4.50% | 5.00% |
| **Above 6 Months to 9 Months** | 5.50% | 6.00% |
| **Above 9 Months to 15 Months** | 6.25% | 6.75% |
| **Above 15 Months to 2 Years** | 6.30% | 6.80% |
| **Above 2 Years to 3 Years** | 6.45% | 6.95% |
| **Above 3 Years to 5 Years** | 6.50% | 7.10% |
| **Above 5 Years to 10 Years** | 6.50% | 7.00% |

### ICICI Bank iWish (Goal-Based Flexible Savings)
*   **Concept:** A highly flexible, digitized recurring deposit variant designed for modern, variable cash flows.
*   **Key Feature:** Unlike traditional RDs that levy strict late fees for missed payments, the iWish account allows depositors to save **variable amounts periodically** at their own convenience.
*   **Advantage:** Depositors can skip months, pay multiple times in a single month, or change their savings velocity without any missed-installment penalties, while earning competitive interest yields up to **7.10% p.a.**

### Delightful Deposits
*   **Concept:** Special RD schemes established in collaboration with leading retail partners.
*   **Key Partners:** Partnerships include major retail giants such as **Croma, MakeMyTrip, Tanishq, and Thomas Cook**.
*   **Advantage:** Allows customers to systematically accumulate savings towards a specific purchase goal (e.g., jewelry, holidays, electronics) and receive specialized partner vouchers, discounts, or top-ups upon maturity.

## 3. Crucial Policy Rules & Penalty Guidelines

### Late RD Installment Penalty
*   A strict penalty of **₹12 per ₹1,000 per month** is levied for late or missed monthly RD installments.
*   **Calculation Rule:** Any fraction or part of a month is counted as a full month for the purpose of penalty calculation.
*   **Deduction:** The cumulative late fees are calculated and directly deducted from the total interest accumulated upon maturity.

### Premature Withdrawal Guidelines
*   **Early Grace Period:** No interest is payable on Recurring Deposits prematurely closed **within 7 days** from the date of opening. However, **no premature closure charges** are applicable within this 7-day window.
*   **Penal Interest Rate Deductions (Deposits < ₹5 Crore):**
    *   *Tenor < 1 Year:* A premature closure penalty of **0.50%** is deducted from the applicable contracted rate.
    *   *Tenor of 1 Year to < 5 Years:* A premature closure penalty of **1.00%** is deducted.
    *   *Tenor of 5 Years and Above:* A premature closure penalty of **1.00%** is deducted.
