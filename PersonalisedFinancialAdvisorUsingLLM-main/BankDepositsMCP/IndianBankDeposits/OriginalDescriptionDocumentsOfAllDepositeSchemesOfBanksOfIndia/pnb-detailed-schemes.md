# Punjab National Bank (PNB) - Original Scheme Description Documents

This document provides the consolidated, detailed specifications, rules, and terms-and-conditions for the complete portfolio of special and standard deposit schemes offered by Punjab National Bank (PNB).

---

## 1. PNB Sugam Term Deposit Scheme (High Liquidity FD)

### Description & Objectives
The **PNB Sugam** scheme is a unique liquid fixed deposit product that addresses the biggest drawback of traditional FDs: the loss of high interest upon premature closure. It allows depositors to withdraw parts of their principal as needed while keeping the remainder untouched and compounding at the originally contracted high interest rate.

### Operational Rules & Specifications
*   **Eligible Customers:** Individuals (singly or jointly), minors aged 10 and above (with proof of age), blind or illiterate persons, partnership firms, proprietorships, companies, HUFs, clubs, societies, and trusts.
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹10,000.
*   **Maximum Deposit:** Below ₹10 Lakhs per customer. *(Note: Separate NRE Rupee Sugam and NRO Sugam versions are available for Non-Resident Indians with no upper limit on NRE).*
*   **Tenure Range:** From **46 Days to 120 Months** (even for incomplete quarters).

### Partial Withdrawal & Premature Rules
*   **Exempt from Breakage Penalties:** The depositor has the facility to withdraw any amount before maturity in **multiples of ₹1** (subject to a minimum partial withdrawal of **₹1,000 at a time**).
*   **No breaking of the entire deposit:** The remaining balance continues to fetch the originalmente contracted interest rate.
*   **Zero Penalty on Part-Withdrawal:** No premature closure penalty is levied on the partially withdrawn portion, provided the residual balance in the Sugam account does not fall below the mandatory threshold of **₹10,000**.
*   **Full Breakage:** If the customer decides to break the entire deposit prematurely, standard contract breakage rules apply (1.00% interest penalty).

## 2. PNB Sugam Plus / Special Term Deposit (444 Days)

### Description & Objectives
The **PNB Sugam Plus (444 Days)** is a promotional, high-yield time deposit scheme designed to attract retail investments during specific interest rate peaks. It has a rigid lock-in and a highly optimized rate structure.

### Key Specifications
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹10,000 (and thereafter in multiples of ₹1,000).
*   **Maximum Deposit:** Up to ₹10 Crore per customer.
*   **Tenure:** Exactly 444 Days.
*   **Auto-Renewal:** Defaults to a standard 12-month auto-renewal period upon maturity if no contrary instructions are received.
*   **Partial Withdrawal:** Strictly **not allowed** (distinguishing it from the standard *PNB Sugam*).

### Interest Rate Structure (w.e.f. June 1, 2026)
*   **General Public:** Baseline peak rate of **6.60% p.a.**
*   **Senior Citizens (60-79 Years):** Eligible for an additional **50 bps premium**, yielding **7.10% p.a.**
*   **Super Senior Citizens (80+):** Eligible for an additional **80 bps premium**, yielding **7.40% p.a.**

### Premature Withdrawal Penalty
*   A **1.00% penal interest** is levied on premature cancellation of the account.
*   The effective rate paid is the contracted rate minus 1.00%, or the card rate applicable for the period the deposit actually ran minus 1.00%, whichever is lower.
*   Penalty is waived if the funds are immediately reinvested into another term deposit scheme of PNB that has a longer remaining tenure than the original contract.

## 3. PNB Tax Saver / Tax Shield Fixed Deposit Scheme

### Description & Objectives
The **PNB Tax Shield** scheme is designed in accordance with Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961. It provides dual benefits: guaranteed long-term interest accrual and tax deductions on annual taxable income.

### Key Specifications
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹100 or in multiples thereof.
*   **Maximum Deposit:** Up to **₹1.50 Lakhs** per financial year.
*   **Lock-in Period:** Mandatory lock-in of **5 Years (60 Months)**.
*   **Premature Withdrawal:** Strictly **not permitted** under any circumstances during the 5-year lock-in period.
*   **Collateral Suitability:** The deposit receipt **cannot** be pledged as collateral for demand loans, overdrafts, or credit cards during the lock-in period.

## 4. PNB Multi-Benefit Term Deposit Scheme

### Description & Objectives
The **PNB Multi-Benefit** scheme is PNB's standard cumulative compounding term deposit. It is aimed at depositors looking for a simple, secure reinvestment plan where interest is systematically reinvested to benefit from the power of quarterly compounding.

### Key Specifications
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹10,000.
*   **Tenure:** Minimum 6 Months to a Maximum of 120 Months.
*   **Compounding Rest:** Interest is compounded on a **quarterly basis** (with quarterly compounding rests) and added to the principal.
*   **Payout:** The accumulated principal and compounded interest are paid as a lump sum upon maturity.
*   **Overdraft/Loan Facility:** Demand loans or overdraft facilities are available up to **90% of the accumulated deposit balance** on written request.

## 5. PNB Special Recurring Deposit Product

### Description & Objectives
The **PNB Special Recurring Deposit** is a B2B corporate systematic savings product designed for government departments, institutions, PSUs, and private corporate bodies. It allows organizations to establish structured welfare-oriented recurring savings accounts on behalf of their employees.

### Operational Mechanics & Rules
*   **Account Structure:** The account is opened in the name of the employing institution, with individual sub-accounts mapping to the name of each specific employee.
*   **Eligibility Threshold:** 
    *   The institution must open the accounts with a minimum of **10 employees**, or
    *   At least **75% of their eligible employee workforce** (whichever is lower).
*   **Installment Frequency:** Monthly installments are deposited directly by the employing institution via payroll deduction or standing instructions.
*   **Delayed Installment Rules:** Delayed monthly payments are subject to standard PNB RD penalties:
    *   A late fee of **₹1 per ₹100** overdue per month is charged on delayed installments.
    *   If installments remain pending at maturity, the interest is calculated on daily balances with half-yearly compounding.
