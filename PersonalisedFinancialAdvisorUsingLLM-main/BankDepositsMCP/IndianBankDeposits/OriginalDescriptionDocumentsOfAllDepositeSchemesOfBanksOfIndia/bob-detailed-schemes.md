# Bank of Baroda (BoB) Detailed Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

Bank of Baroda (BoB) offers structured public sector deposit portfolios, balancing highly secure standard terms with yield-maximized non-callable bulk placements and systematically structured micro-savings plans.

## 1. Baroda Advantage Fixed Deposits (Non-Callable)
The Baroda Advantage Fixed Deposit is a non-callable domestic term deposit product where the depositor receives a preferential interest rate premium in exchange for surrendering the option of premature withdrawal.

### A. Policy Guidelines & Rules
*   **Minimum Deposit Amount:** ₹1,00,01,000 (₹1 Crore and one thousand rupees), and in multiples of ₹1,000 thereafter.
*   **Maximum Deposit Limit:** Below ₹3 Crore for retail premium brackets (no upper limit for corporate bulk).
*   **Tenure Range:** 12 months to 120 months.
*   **Preferential Yield Premium:** Offers **15 to 25 bps higher interest rate** compared to normal callable fixed deposits of identical tenures.
*   **Interest Pay-out Options:** Cumulative (reinvestment compounding), quarterly payout, or monthly discounted payout.
*   **Premature Withdrawal Restraints:** **Strictly not allowed** under any circumstances, except in extreme regulatory scenarios or death of the primary depositor.
*   **Loan/Overdraft Restrictions:** Loan/overdraft facilities are **not available** against the security of Non-Callable Deposits.
*   **Auto-Renewal:** In the absence of written instructions, deposits of less than ₹3 Crore are auto-renewed for a default period.

## 2. bob Systematic Deposit Plan (SDP)
The bob Systematic Deposit Plan is Bank of Baroda's core recurring deposit product, designed to cultivate monthly financial discipline with yields comparable to standard FDs.

### A. Key Specifications
*   **Minimum Monthly Contribution:**
    *   *Rural and Semi-Urban Branches:* Low entry threshold of **₹50 per month**.
    *   *Urban and Metro Branches:* **₹100 per month**.
*   **Tenure Range:** 6 months to 120 months (10 years).
*   **Interest Compounding:** Compounded quarterly at card rates applicable to the corresponding term.
*   **Senior Citizen Premium:** Seniors receive an additional **50 bps** on retail deposits below ₹3 Crore, reaching peak yields of up to **7.65% p.a.** on designated promotional terms.
*   **Overdue Installment Policy:** Standard public sector penalties apply for delayed installments, calculated based on the number of overdue days at the time of maturity settlement.

## 3. bob earth Green Term Deposits
The bob earth Green Term Deposit scheme is dedicated to raising retail funds for financing eco-friendly initiatives, renewable energy installations, and sustainable development projects.

### A. Key Specifications
*   **Tenure Options:** Specific green tenors of **1111 days, 1717 days, and 2200 days**.
*   **Eligibility:** Resident individuals, HUFs, partnership firms, companies, trusts, and corporate bodies.
*   **Rate of Interest:** Competitive yields priced slightly adjusted or at par with standard card rates.
*   **Premature Withdrawal:** Permitted under standard callable rules (subject to 1% penalty guidelines).

---

## 4. Premature Breakage Policy (Callable Deposits)
For all standard callable deposits below ₹3 Crore:
*   **Deduction Penalty:** A **1.00% penalty** is applied for premature closure.
*   **Final Interest Calculation:** The effective interest rate payable will be **1.00% below the rate ruling** on the date of deposit for the period for which the deposit actually ran, or 1.00% below the contracted rate, whichever is lower.
