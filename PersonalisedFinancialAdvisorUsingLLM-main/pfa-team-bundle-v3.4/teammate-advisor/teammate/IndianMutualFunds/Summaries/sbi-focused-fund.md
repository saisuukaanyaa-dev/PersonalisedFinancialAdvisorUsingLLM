# SBI Focused Fund (Direct Growth)

## 📌 Executive Summary
**SBI Focused Fund** is a high-conviction equity scheme managed by **SBI Mutual Fund**. The fund follows a concentrated investment approach, holding a tight portfolio of a maximum of 30 stocks across market capitalizations to maximize alpha generation while adhering to rigorous risk management guidelines.
