# Gold (Physical, ETF, Sovereign Gold Bonds, Digital)

**What it is:** An inflation hedge and portfolio diversifier. Available as physical gold (jewelry/coins), Gold ETFs (paper gold tracking gold price), Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs — government-issued, pays interest plus tracks gold price), or digital gold.

**Typical returns:** Historically 8-10% CAGR over long periods, but volatile and driven by macro factors (inflation, currency, geopolitical risk) rather than fundamentals like earnings.

**Liquidity:** Gold ETFs are highly liquid (traded on exchange). SGBs have a lock-in (typically 8 years, with early exit windows after year 5) but can be traded on exchanges too, often at a discount to spot. Physical gold has making-charge/purity drag on resale.

**Risk:** Medium. Low correlation with equities, which is its main portfolio value — it tends to do well when equities do poorly.

**Taxation:** SGBs — capital gains at maturity are tax-exempt (India); interest is taxable. Gold ETFs follow debt-fund-like taxation. Physical gold has additional making charges and purity risk.

**Best for:** Portfolio diversification (typically 5-15% allocation), hedging against inflation/currency risk. Not ideal as the core of a growth portfolio since it doesn't generate earnings/cash flow the way equity does.
