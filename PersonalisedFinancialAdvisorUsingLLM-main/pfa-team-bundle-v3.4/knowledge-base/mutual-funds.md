# Mutual Funds (Active)

**What it is:** A pooled investment vehicle managed by a professional fund manager, investing in a mix of equity, debt, or hybrid instruments per the fund's stated mandate.

**Types relevant to goal planning:** Equity funds (large/mid/small cap), debt funds (short/medium/long duration), hybrid/balanced funds, ELSS (tax-saving, India-specific, 3-year lock-in).

**Typical returns:** Varies widely by category and fund manager skill; equity funds historically 10-15% CAGR long-term, debt funds 6-8%, hybrid in between. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results — always caveat this explicitly.

**Liquidity:** High for open-ended funds (redeem any business day, settlement in 1-3 days), except ELSS during its lock-in.

**Risk:** Depends entirely on the underlying category — equity funds carry equity risk, debt funds carry interest-rate and credit risk.

**Taxation:** Same framework as ETFs/index funds by category (equity-oriented vs debt-oriented) — verify current rules.

**Best for:** Investors who want active management and are willing to pay a higher expense ratio (typically 1-2%) for the chance of outperformance, or specific mandates (ELSS for tax saving, sectoral bets).
