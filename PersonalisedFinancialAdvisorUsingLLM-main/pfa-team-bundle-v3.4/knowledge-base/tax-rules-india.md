# Tax Rules Reference (India) — verify against current law before quoting specific rates

**Section 80C (old tax regime):** Deductions up to ₹1.5 lakh/year for investments in PPF, ELSS, life insurance premiums, EPF contributions, principal repayment on home loans, and a few others. Not available under the new tax regime.

**Equity LTCG/STCG:** Long-term (holding > 1 year) gains on listed equity/equity mutual funds taxed at a preferential rate above an annual exemption threshold; short-term (≤ 1 year) taxed at a flat higher rate. Rates and thresholds have changed in recent budgets — always confirm current rates via search or a live data source rather than quoting from memory.

**Debt fund taxation:** Since April 2023, gains on debt mutual funds (funds with <35% equity allocation) are taxed at the investor's income slab rate regardless of holding period — indexation benefit was removed. This is a significant change from the pre-2023 rules; be careful not to apply outdated indexation logic.

**FD interest:** Fully taxed at slab rate, added to "income from other sources." TDS deducted if interest exceeds the threshold in a financial year.

**Sovereign Gold Bonds:** Capital gains at maturity (redemption from government) are tax-exempt. Interest earned (typically ~2.5%/year) is taxable at slab rate. Gains on secondary-market sale before maturity are taxed as capital gains.

**PPF/EPF:** EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) status — contributions (up to 80C limit), interest, and maturity proceeds are all tax-free, subject to conditions.

**Disclaimer to always include:** Tax rules change with each Union Budget. This document is a reference starting point, not current tax advice — always recommend the user verify with a tax professional or current government sources before acting.
