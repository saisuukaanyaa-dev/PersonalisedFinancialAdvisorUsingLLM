# Bonds

**What it is:** A debt instrument where the investor lends money to a government or corporation in exchange for periodic interest (coupon) and return of principal at maturity.

**Types:** Government bonds/G-Secs (very low risk), corporate bonds (risk varies by issuer credit rating), tax-free bonds, sovereign gold bonds (a hybrid — see gold.md).

**Typical returns:** Government bonds 6-7.5%, investment-grade corporate bonds 7-9%, higher for lower-rated (higher risk) corporate bonds.

**Liquidity:** Varies. Government bonds and highly-rated corporate bonds traded on exchanges are more liquid; many bonds are best held to maturity.

**Risk:** Low (government) to medium-high (lower-rated corporate). Interest rate risk affects bond prices inversely — rising rates reduce the market value of existing bonds.

**Taxation:** Interest income taxed at slab rate; capital gains on sale before maturity follow debt taxation rules.

**Best for:** Medium-term goals (3-7 years), income-seeking investors, diversifying away from pure equity risk, capital preservation with modestly higher yield than FDs.
