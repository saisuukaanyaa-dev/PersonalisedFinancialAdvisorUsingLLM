# NIFTY 50 Historical Example

**Category:** dataset_methodology

## Instruction
What is the difference between a full-year and partial-year NIFTY 50 member?

## Context
{"full_year_member": true, "partial_year_example": true}

## Response
A full-year member was a NIFTY 50 constituent for the entire calendar year. A partial-year member entered or left the index during the year, so its reported membership-period performance covers only the applicable dates.
