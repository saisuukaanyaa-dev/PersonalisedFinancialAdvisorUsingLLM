## 2. Comprehensive Breakdown of Individual Schemes

### 🔵 A. HDFC Bank HealthCover FD
An innovative hybrid deposit product that provides dual benefits of wealth accumulation and emergency health coverage.
*   **How it works:** In addition to standard compounding interest on your FD, the bank bundles a **Critical Illness Insurance Cover** (partnered with HDFC ERGO) at no extra premium.
*   **Insurance Value:** Coverage amount is equal to the FD principal (up to a maximum of ₹10 Lakhs) for depositors aged 18 to 50 years.
*   **Tenure Requirement:** Minimum tenure of **1 Year**.
*   **Premature Withdrawal:** Permitted, but if the FD is broken, the bundled insurance policy is automatically terminated.

### 🔵 B. Regular Fixed Deposit (Cumulative & Non-Cumulative)
The traditional lump-sum investment model available to residents, HUFs, and corporate bodies.
*   **Payout Options:**
    *   *Cumulative:* Compounded quarterly and paid at maturity.
    *   *Non-Cumulative:* Payout of interest at monthly (discounted rate) or quarterly intervals, ideal for retirees or regular cash flow requirements.
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹5,000.

### 🔵 C. Non-Withdrawable Term Deposits
Designed for depositors seeking maximum yields who are certain they do not require liquidity before the maturity date.
*   **Deposit Threshold:** Generally applicable to deposits of ₹2 Crore and above.
*   **Feature:** Offers a premium interest rate over the callable domestic FDs. Premature withdrawal or partial break is strictly prohibited.

### 🔵 D. FCNR (Foreign Currency Non-Resident) Deposits
Allows Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) to maintain foreign currency deposits in India without exchange rate risk.
*   **Supported Currencies:** USD, GBP, EUR, YEN, AUD, CAD, SGD.
*   **Feature Rates:** Competitive yields (up to **6.25% p.a. on USD** tenures) which are completely tax-exempt in India.
*   **Tenure range:** 1 Year to 5 Years.

### 🔵 E. Overdraft Against Fixed Deposit (Super Saver Facility)
Provides instant liquidity without the need to break the fixed deposit and lose out on accrued interest.
*   **Limit:** Up to **90% of the FD principal** value.
*   **Interest Spread:** Interest is charged only on the utilized overdraft amount, typically priced at a low spread of **1% to 2% over the FD interest rate**.
*   **Process:** Fully digital activation via NetBanking.

### 🔵 F. Five Year Tax Saving Fixed Deposit
A dedicated tax shelter program aligned with national savings mandates.
*   **Tenure:** Exactly 5 Years (lock-in).
*   **Tax Benefit:** Deductions up to ₹1.5 Lakhs under Section 80C. No premature sweep or withdrawal is allowed.

## 3. Recurring Deposit (RD) Schemes

HDFC Bank's systematic savings plans allow depositors to build a robust corpus through periodic investments.

### 🔵 A. Standard Recurring Deposit
*   **Tenure Range:** 6 months to 120 months (in multiples of 3 months).
*   **Installment Limit:** Minimum **₹1,000 monthly** (and in multiples of ₹100 thereafter).
*   **Interest Rates:**
    *   **1 Year to 2 Years:** **6.60% p.a.** (General) | **7.10% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **2 Years to 3 Years:** **7.10% p.a.** (General) | **7.60% p.a.** (Senior)
*   **Compounding:** Interest is compounded quarterly.
*   **Delay Penalties:** If monthly installments are missed, the bank levies a penalty rate on delayed installments.

### 🔵 B. My Passion Fund (Flexible Goal RD)
A highly flexible variant of the standard RD designed for customizable, goal-oriented savings.
*   **Feature:** Allows depositors to top-up their monthly savings anytime during the tenure to accelerate reaching their target goal.
*   **Deposits:** Can deposit multiple installments in a single month up to a maximum cap.
