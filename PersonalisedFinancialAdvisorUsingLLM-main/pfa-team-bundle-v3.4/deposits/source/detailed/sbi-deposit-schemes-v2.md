# State Bank of India (SBI) Detailed Deposit Schemes (September 2026) - v2

State Bank of India (SBI), the country's largest public sector bank, provides a comprehensive suite of fixed deposit (FD) and recurring deposit (RD) schemes. These schemes are meticulously designed to cater to various savings goals, liquidity needs, and demographic profiles.

## 2. Comprehensive Breakdown of Individual Schemes

### 🟢 A. Amrit Vrishti (444 Days Special Scheme)
A highly popular, specific-tenor promotional scheme designed to capture peak yield opportunities in the medium-term bracket.
*   **Tenure:** Exactly 444 days.
*   **Interest Rates:**
    *   **General Public:** **7.25% p.a.** (revised to baseline card rate of **6.45% p.a.** w.e.f. Dec 15, 2025, but promotional premium buckets exist).
    *   **Senior Citizens:** **7.75% p.a.**
    *   **Super Senior Citizens (80+):** **7.85% p.a.**
*   **Maturity/Subscription Validity:** Open for subscription until **March 31, 2025** (with potential extensions).
*   **Compounding:** Interest is compounded quarterly and can be paid on maturity or in monthly/quarterly intervals as opted.

### 🟢 B. SBI WeCare (Senior Citizen Special FD)
An exclusive long-term fixed deposit scheme created to protect the interest income of senior citizens during volatile rate cycles.
*   **Tenure:** 5 years to 10 years.
*   **Eligibility:** Senior citizens aged 60 years and above.
*   **Rate Premium:** Offers an additional premium of **50 bps** over and above the regular card rate (yielding **7.05% p.a.** under latest revisions).
*   **Payment Frequency:** Available under both cumulative and non-cumulative (monthly/quarterly interest payout) plans.

### 🟢 C. SBI Patrons (Super Senior Benefit Program)
A specialized benefit program created to reward the eldest demographic.
*   **Eligibility:** Super Senior Citizens aged 80 years and above.
*   **Rate Premium:** Entitles the depositor to an additional **10 bps premium** over and above the applicable senior citizen interest rate across all tenures.
*   **Exclusions:** This scheme does not apply to Recurring Deposits, Green Rupee Term Deposits, Tax Savings Schemes, Multi-Option Deposits (MODS), Capital Gains Accounts, and Non-Callable deposits.

### 🟢 D. Sarvottam (Non-Callable Domestic Retail Term Deposit)
A high-yield deposit scheme tailored for savers with bulk funds who do not require liquidity before maturity.
*   **Deposit Window:** From **₹1.0001 Crore to less than ₹3 Crore**.
*   **Lock-in Feature:** No premature withdrawal is permitted under any circumstances.
*   **Interest Rates (Compounded Quarterly):**
    *   **1 Year:** **6.55% p.a.** (General Public) | **7.05% p.a.** (Senior Citizens)
    *   **2 Years:** **6.80% p.a.** (General Public) | **7.30% p.a.** (Senior Citizens)
*   **Yield Premium:** Provides a substantial 30 bps to 40 bps premium over callable standard card rates.

### 🟢 E. SBI Green Rupee Term Deposit (SGRTD)
An environmentally conscious deposit scheme where funds are exclusively utilized to finance certified green projects (renewable energy, clean transport, green buildings).
*   **Tenures:** Three specific tenors of **1111, 1777, and 2222 days**.
*   **Interest Rates:** Priced at **10 bps below the standard card rate** for retail domestic deposits. Available to residents, NRIs, and institutional depositors.

### 🟢 F. Reinvestment Plan (Special Term Deposit)
A variation of the standard Fixed Deposit where interest is compounded quarterly and paid only at maturity, maximizing the compound interest multiplier.
*   **Tenure:** 6 months to 10 years.
*   **Minimum Principal:** ₹1,000.

### 🟢 G. Annuity Deposit Scheme
Enables a depositor to invest a lump-sum amount and receive equal monthly payouts comprising a portion of the principal plus reducing interest.
*   **Tenure:** Available for 3, 5, 7, and 10 years.
*   **Payout Structure:** Starts one month after deposit. Ideal for pension-like regular income.
*   **Minimum Deposit:** Based on a minimum annuity payout of ₹1,000 per month.

### 🟢 H. Multi Option Deposit Scheme (MODS)
A highly liquid term deposit linked directly to the depositor's savings or current account.
*   **How it works:** When the linked transactional account balance falls below a threshold, the MODS deposit is automatically broken in multiple of **₹1,000** to honor checks or ATM transactions, avoiding overdraft fees.
*   **Interest:** Unbroken units continue to earn the higher term deposit interest rate contracted at inception.

### 🟢 I. SBI Tax Savings Scheme-2006
Designed to provide tax exemption under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.
*   **Tenure:** Minimum lock-in of **5 years** up to 10 years.
*   **Lock-in Rule:** No premature withdrawal or loan facility is permitted during the 5-year lock-in.
*   **Limits:** Minimum deposit ₹100; maximum ₹1.50 Lakhs per financial year.

## 3. Recurring Deposit (RD) Scheme
SBI's systematic recurring savings plan instills financial discipline with stable growth.

*   **Tenure Range:** 12 months (1 year) to 120 months (10 years) in multiples of 3 months.
*   **Installment Limits:** Minimum **₹100 per month**; no maximum limit.
*   **Interest Rates (Compounded Quarterly):**
    *   **1 Year to 2 Years:** **6.80% p.a.** (General) | **7.30% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **2 Years to 3 Years:** **7.00% p.a.** (General) | **7.50% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **3 Years to 5 Years:** **6.50% to 6.75% p.a.**
*   **Delay Penalties:** Delayed monthly installments attract a penalty based on tenure (typically ₹1.50 per ₹100 per month for tenures up to 5 years, and ₹2.00 per ₹100 per month for tenures exceeding 5 years).
