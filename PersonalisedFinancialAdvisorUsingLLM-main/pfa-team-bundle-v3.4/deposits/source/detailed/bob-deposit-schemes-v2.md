## 2. Comprehensive Breakdown of Individual Schemes

### 📊 A. Baroda Advantage Fixed Deposit (Non-Callable)
A premium high-yield retail term deposit designed for depositors who do not require short-term liquidity.
*   **Deposit Window:** From **₹1.0001 Crore to less than ₹3 Crore** (also available for bulk values up to ₹10 Crore).
*   **Core Rule:** Premature withdrawal or partial break of non-callable deposits is strictly prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
*   **Advantage:** Offers differential interest rate premiums (typically 10 bps to 25 bps higher) compared to standard callable fixed deposits of the same tenures.
*   **Loans:** No loan or overdraft facility is allowed against the security of Baroda Advantage non-callable deposits.

### 📊 B. Systematic Deposit Plan ("bob SDP")
Bank of Baroda's structured recurring deposit product, designed to promote disciplined saving habits.
*   **Installment Limits:**
    *   *Rural & Semi-Urban Branches:* Minimum installment of **₹50 per month**.
    *   *Urban & Metro Branches:* Minimum installment of **₹100 per month**.
*   **Tenure Range:** 6 months to 120 months (10 years).
*   **Interest compounding:** Compounded quarterly and credited at maturity.
*   **Senior Citizen Benefit:** Senior citizens (60+) receive an additional **0.50% p.a. premium** on deposits below ₹1 Crore.

### 📊 C. Baroda Tax Savings Fixed Deposit
An exclusive tax shelter deposit aligned with Section 80C.
*   **Tenure:** Exactly 5 Years (lock-in).
*   **Interest Rates w.e.f. May 15, 2026:**
    *   **General Public:** **6.30% p.a.**
    *   **Senior Citizens:** **6.90% p.a.**
    *   **Super Senior Citizens (80+):** **7.00% p.a.**
*   **Rule:** Lock-in is absolute; no premature break is permitted during the 5-year tenure.

### 📊 D. bob earth Green Term Deposits
Eco-friendly fixed deposits designed to finance clean energy, sustainable agriculture, and pollution control initiatives.
*   **Feature:** Available in both callable and non-callable options up to ₹10 Crore, supporting sustainable development goals with green-linked interest payouts.
