## 2. Comprehensive Breakdown of Individual Schemes

### 🍯 A. Nitya Nidhi Deposit (NND) Scheme (Daily Pigmy Collection)
A legendary daily door-to-door micro-savings collection scheme designed specifically for daily wage earners, small traders, and vendors.
*   **How it works:** Merges the Pigmy Deposit Scheme of the erstwhile Syndicate Bank with Canara Bank's NNND Scheme. The bank assigns an **Authorized Agent** who visits your doorstep daily (or at less frequent intervals) to collect your savings.
*   **Deposit Limits:** Minimum contribution of **₹50 per month**. The daily collection cannot exceed **₹1,000 per day** (maximum ₹30,000 in a month).
*   **Tenure:** Exactly **63 Months** (5.25 years).
*   **Interest Yield (w.e.f October 1, 2022):** **2.00% p.a.** on maturity.
*   **Loan Facility:** Depositors can borrow up to **75% of the outstanding NND balance** as a loan.
*   **Premature Closure Rules:** Early closure is permitted subject to a tiered interest payout:
    *   *Less than 12 months:* Receives **0.10% interest**, subject to a penalty recovery of **0.05%** on the interest payable.
    *   *12 to 24 months:* Receives **0.25% interest** (no penalty).
    *   *24 to 39 months:* Receives **0.50% interest** (no penalty).
    *   *39 to 63 months:* Receives **1.00% interest** (no penalty).

### 🍯 B. Kamadhenu Deposit (Reinvestment Plan)
A core compounding fixed deposit product where accrued interest is added back to the principal quarterly.
*   **Compounding:** Quarterly compounded interest paid as a cumulative lump-sum at maturity. Offers a much higher effective yield over standard simple-interest FDs.
*   **Minimum Deposit:** ₹1,000.

### 🍯 C. Canara Dhanvarsha (Flexible Recurring Deposit)
A flexible-installment recurring deposit scheme tailored for savers with fluctuating monthly incomes.
*   **Core Feature:** Allows depositors to make **more than one installment deposit during a single month**. Multiple deposits can be made in multiples of regular installments. No penalty is levied for delayed or skipped payments.
*   **Deposit Limits:** Minimum deposit of **₹500 per month** up to a maximum of **₹1,00,000 per month**.
*   **Tenure:** 1 Year to 10 Years (in multiples of 3 months).
*   **Loan Facility:** Up to **90% of the accumulated deposit balance** can be borrowed.

### 🍯 D. Canara Green Deposit
An eco-friendly fixed deposit scheme. The funds collected under this program are earmarked for green lending, including solar housing, waste-to-energy, and electric mobility projects.

### 🍯 E. Canara Tax Saver Scheme
Provides tax deductions under Section 80C. 5-year lock-in with a maximum limit of ₹1.5 Lakhs.

## 3. Standard Recurring Deposit (RD) Scheme
Allows systematic, disciplined savings.

*   **Tenure Range:** 6 months to 120 months (10 years) in multiples of 3 months.
*   **Compounding:** Interest is compounded quarterly.
*   **Premature Withdrawal Penalty:** Attracts a **1.00% premature closure penalty** on deposits opened on or after Feb 4, 2011. Interest is paid at 1.00% below the applicable card rate for the period the deposit was held.
