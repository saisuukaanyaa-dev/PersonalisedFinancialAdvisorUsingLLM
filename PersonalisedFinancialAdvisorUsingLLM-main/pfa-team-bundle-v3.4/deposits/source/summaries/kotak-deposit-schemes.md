# Kotak Mahindra Bank Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

Kotak Mahindra Bank offers competitive interest rates on standard and senior citizen deposit products, alongside a streamlined online platform for automated monthly savings.

## Fixed Deposits & Recurring Deposit Interest Rates
*(For Retail Deposits below ₹3 Crore, w.e.f. mid 2025 / September 2026)*

Kotak Mahindra Bank offers robust short-to-medium-term yields, allowing depositors to secure guaranteed growth on recurring allocations:

* **Short-Term Deposits (6 to 18 Months):** General citizen rates range from **5.50% to 6.70% p.a.**, whereas Senior Citizens earn **6.00% to 7.20% p.a.**
* **Medium-Term (1 Year to 2 Years):** Yields stand at **7.10% p.a.** for standard depositors and **7.60% p.a.** for Senior Citizens.
* **Longer-Term (2 Years to 3 Years):** Yields stand at **7.00% p.a.** for standard depositors and **7.50% p.a.** for Senior Citizens.
* **Standard Ceiling Rates:** Standard citizen yields peak at **7.20% p.a.** and senior citizen yields peak at **7.70% p.a.** across selected mid-range tenors.

## Recurring Deposit (RD) Key Features & Rules

Kotak RD accounts help build disciplined financial reserves with automated transfers directly linked to your savings account.

* **Minimum Monthly Deposit:** The minimum entry threshold is **₹100 per month**.
* **Flexible Tenures:** Available for a minimum of **6 months** and up to **120 months** (10 years), customizable in multiples of 3 months.
* **Maximum Monthly Installment Limits:**
  * For tenures **up to 36 months**, the maximum monthly deposit limit is **₹1,00,000**.
  * For tenures **greater than 36 months**, the maximum monthly deposit limit is restricted to **₹25,000**.
* **Late Payment Policy:** A late payment penalty of **2% p.a.** is levied on missed or delayed recurring installments for the exact overdue period.
* **Premature Closure:** Early withdrawal is allowed, subject to premature penal rules. No interest is payable on accounts closed within the first few weeks of opening.
* **TDS Compliance:** Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) applies to accumulated interest as per prevailing income tax guidelines once threshold limits are exceeded.
* **Ease of Access:** Seamlessly integrate, track, and regularize your RD installments online via Kotak Net Banking or the Kotak Mobile App.
