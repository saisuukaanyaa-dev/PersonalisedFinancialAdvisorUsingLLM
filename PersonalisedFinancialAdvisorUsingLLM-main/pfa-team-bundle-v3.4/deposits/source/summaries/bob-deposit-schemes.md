# Bank of Baroda (BoB) Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

Bank of Baroda (BoB) provides structured systematic deposit plans under its specialized "bob SDP" program, enabling general public and senior citizen depositors to grow a guaranteed corpus.

## Fixed Deposits & Recurring Deposit Interest Rates
*(For Retail Deposits below ₹3 Crore, w.e.f. mid 2025 / September 1, 2026)*

The deposit rates are tiered progressively across distinct maturity periods, supporting quarterly compounding on systematic plans:

## Recurring Deposit (bob SDP) Features & Rules

The Systematic Deposit Plan is designed to promote disciplined saving habits with attractive banking privileges.

* **Minimum Monthly Installment:** Start saving with **₹50** in rural and semi-urban branches, and **₹100** in urban and metropolitan branches.
* **Flexible Tenures:** Open an RD from **6 months to 120 months** (10 years) in standard multiples.
* **Compounding Power:** Interest is compounded **quarterly**, providing a compounding advantage over simple monthly savings.
* **Delayed Installments:** Missed installments must be resolved prior to maturity. Standard interest adjustments apply upon final withdrawal.
* **Premature Withdrawal:** In case of premature closure, a **1.00% penal deduction** is applied to the applicable interest rate for the period the funds remained with the bank.
* **Preferential Pricing:** Senior citizens receive an additional **50 bps** on all deposits under ₹1 Crore, while Super Seniors are eligible for a **10 bps premium** above the senior citizen card rate on specific medium-to-long tenures.
