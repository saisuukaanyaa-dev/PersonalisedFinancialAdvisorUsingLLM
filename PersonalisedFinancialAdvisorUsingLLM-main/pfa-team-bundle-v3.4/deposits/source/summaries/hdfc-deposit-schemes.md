# HDFC Bank Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

HDFC Bank, India's largest private sector bank, provides highly competitive deposit schemes with premium promotional brackets and bundled wellness protections.

## 1. Fixed Deposits (FDs)
HDFC Bank offers diverse retail fixed deposits with customized options for liquid, tax-saving, and premium investments.

### Interest Rates
*   **Standard Rates:** Range from **2.75% to 6.50% p.a.** for the general public, and **3.25% to 7.00% p.a.** for senior citizens.
*   **Peak Promotional Rates:** Offered on specific high-yield tenures of **35-month** and **55-month** deposits:
    *   **General Public:** **7.20% p.a.**
    *   **Senior Citizens:** **7.70% p.a.**

### Unique FD Variations
*   **HDFC Bank HealthCover FD:** An innovative hybrid scheme that pairs a traditional fixed deposit with a critical illness medical insurance policy, enabling depositors to grow their wealth while securing medical coverage.
*   **FCNR (Foreign Currency Non-Resident) Deposits:** Offered in major currencies (USD, GBP, EUR, Yen, SGD, CAD, AUD) for global investors.
*   **Five-Year Tax Saving FD:** Lock-in term of 5 years to claim tax deductions under section 80C.

## 2. Recurring Deposits (RDs)
Disciplined systematic savings plans spanning tenures from 6 months to 120 months.
*   **Standard RD Rates:** Range from **4.50% to 7.25% p.a.** for regular citizens and up to **7.75% p.a.** for senior citizens.
*   **Key Tenures:**
    *   **1 Year to 2 Years:** **6.60% p.a.** (General) | **7.10% p.a.** (Senior)
    *   **2 Years to 3 Years:** **7.10% p.a.** (General) | **7.60% p.a.** (Senior)
*   **Minimum Entry Deposit:** **₹1,000 monthly** (Standard).
