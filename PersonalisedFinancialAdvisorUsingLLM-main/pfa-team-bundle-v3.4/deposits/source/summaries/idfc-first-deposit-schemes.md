IDFC FIRST Bank features some of the most customer-centric and highly rewarding retail term deposit products in India, highlighted by industry-leading interest rates and zero-penalty guidelines.

## Recurring Deposit (RD) Key Features & Rules (Differentiating Benefits)

IDFC FIRST Bank stands out due to its extremely progressive, consumer-friendly policies that eliminate common pain points of systematic saving.

* **Zero Penalty for Missed Installments:** Understanding that emergencies happen, IDFC FIRST Bank **does not charge any penalty** for missed or delayed recurring deposit installments.
* **Start Small:** Instantly book an RD starting with an installment of just **₹100 per month**.
* **Flexible Tenures:** Lock-in systematic savings cleanly across selective key tenures of 6, 9, 12, 24, and 36 months.
* **Zero Premature Penalty for Seniors:** A premature closure penalty of 1% applies to standard accounts opened or renewed on or after May 2, 2019. However, **Senior Citizens are completely exempt** from any premature closure penalties.
* **Compounding Advantage:** Interest is calculated using standard quarterly compounding, maximizing the terminal maturity value of your accumulated savings.
* **Instant Digital Setup:** Book RDs in under a minute with seamless online onboarding through the IDFC FIRST Bank mobile application or internet banking.
