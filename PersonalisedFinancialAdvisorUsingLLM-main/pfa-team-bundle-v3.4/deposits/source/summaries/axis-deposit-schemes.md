# Axis Bank Deposit Schemes (September 2026)

Axis Bank provides tailored retail interest yields structured over highly specific maturity buckets, with high interest steps for short-to-medium-term investors.

## 1. Fixed Deposits (FDs)
*   **Standard Rates:** Range from **3.00% to 6.45% p.a.** for standard citizens, and **3.50% to 7.20% p.a.** for senior citizens on standard tenures (7 days to 10 years).
*   **Peak Promotional Rates:** Peak buckets offer up to **7.20% p.a.** for general depositors on specific tenures.
*   **Tax Saver FD:** A 5-year lock-in fixed deposit for tax saving benefits.
*   **Fixed Deposit Plus:** Tailored for non-callable deposits with a minimum deposit threshold of ₹3 Crore.

## 2. Recurring Deposits (RDs)
Axis Bank RDs support structured savings with tenures ranging from a minimum of 6 months up to a maximum of 10 years.

### Important RD Account Rules
*   **Minimum Deposit:** Broadly ranges from **₹500** to **₹1,000 monthly** depending on specific account programs.
*   **Late Installment Penalty:** Accounts opened on or after August 9, 2016, incur a penalty fee of **₹10 for every ₹1,000 overdue** (calculated monthly).
*   **Premature Withdrawal Penalty:** A premature closure penalty of **1.00% below the contracted rate** is applicable for deposits under ₹5 Crore.
